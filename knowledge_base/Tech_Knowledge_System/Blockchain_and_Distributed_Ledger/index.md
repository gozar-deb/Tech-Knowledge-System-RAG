## Quick Definition
Blockchain and Distributed Ledger Technology (DLT) are decentralized, cryptographically secured, and immutable record-keeping systems. They facilitate transparent and tamper-proof transactions across a network without central authority, forming the foundation for cryptocurrencies and decentralized applications.

## Key Concepts
- Immutability → Data, once recorded, cannot be changed, ensuring integrity.
- Decentralization → No single point of control, distributing power across the network.
- Consensus Mechanisms → Protocols ensuring agreement on the ledger's state among participants.
- Cryptography → Secures transactions and links blocks using hash functions and digital signatures.
- Smart Contracts → Self-executing agreements with code-enforced terms on a blockchain.
- Distributed Ledger → A shared, synchronized database across multiple network participants.
- Tokenization → Representing assets or rights as digital tokens on a blockchain.
