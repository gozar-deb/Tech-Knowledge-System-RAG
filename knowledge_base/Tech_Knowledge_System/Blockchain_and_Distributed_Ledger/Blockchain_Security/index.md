## Quick Definition
Blockchain security refers to the comprehensive measures and protocols designed to protect blockchain networks and their underlying data from cyber threats. It ensures the integrity, privacy, and functionality of decentralized ledgers through cryptographic techniques, robust consensus mechanisms, and secure smart contract practices.

## Key Concepts
- Cryptography → Secures transactions and data integrity using hash functions and public-key encryption.
- Consensus Mechanisms → Algorithms ensuring agreement across distributed nodes to prevent malicious alterations.
- Immutability → Once recorded, transactions are cryptographically linked and cannot be altered or deleted.
- Decentralization → Distributes control and data, eliminating single points of failure and enhancing resilience.
- Smart Contract Security → Focuses on identifying and mitigating vulnerabilities in self-executing code.
- Attack Vectors → Potential entry points for malicious actors to exploit vulnerabilities in a blockchain system.
