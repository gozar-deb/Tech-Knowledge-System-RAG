## Quick Definition
Fourier Analysis is a mathematical method for decomposing complex signals into a sum of simpler sinusoidal components. It transforms a signal from its original domain (e.g., time) into a representation based on frequency, revealing the underlying periodicities and spectral content. This technique is fundamental for signal processing, enabling analysis, filtering, and synthesis of various waveforms.

## Key Concepts
- Fourier Transform → Converts continuous signals to frequency domain.
- Discrete Fourier Transform (DFT) → Converts discrete signals to frequency domain.
- Fast Fourier Transform (FFT) → Efficient algorithm for computing the DFT.
- Frequency Domain → Signal representation showing amplitude and phase of frequencies.
- Time Domain → Signal representation showing amplitude over time.
- Spectral Analysis → Examining frequency content to understand signal characteristics.
- Aliasing → Distortion caused by undersampling a signal.
- Windowing → Applying a function to a finite signal segment to reduce spectral leakage.
