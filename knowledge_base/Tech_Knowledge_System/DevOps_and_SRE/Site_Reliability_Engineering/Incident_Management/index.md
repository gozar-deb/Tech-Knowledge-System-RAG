## Quick Definition
Incident Management is the structured process of detecting, diagnosing, and resolving service disruptions to restore normal operations swiftly. Its primary goal is to minimize impact on users and business functions, ensuring high service availability and quality.

## Key Concepts
- Incident → An unplanned interruption or degradation of an IT service.
- Alerting → Automated notifications signaling potential or actual service problems.
- Escalation → Process of involving specialized teams or higher authority for unresolved incidents.
- Post-Mortem → Blameless review after an incident to learn and prevent recurrence.
- Runbook → Documented procedures for handling routine operational tasks and incidents.
- Observability → The ability to understand a system's internal states from external outputs.
