## Quick Definition
Data sovereignty is the principle that data is governed by the laws of the nation or region where it is created or stored. It dictates legal jurisdiction over data, impacting its storage, processing, and transfer, often driven by privacy, national security, and economic concerns.

## Key Concepts
- Data Residency → Requirement for data to be stored in a specific geographic location.
- Data Localization → Policies mandating data processing and storage within national borders.
- Jurisdiction → Legal authority over data based on its physical location and applicable laws.
- Cross-Border Data Flow → Movement of data across national boundaries, subject to legal restrictions.
- Digital Sovereignty → A nation's control over its digital infrastructure, data, and technology.
- Regulatory Compliance → Adherence to data handling laws like GDPR, CCPA, and other national regulations.
