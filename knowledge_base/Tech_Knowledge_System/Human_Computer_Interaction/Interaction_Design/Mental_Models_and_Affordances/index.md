## Quick Definition
Mental models are the internal representations users construct about how a system operates, based on their experiences. Affordances are the perceived action possibilities of an object, guiding users on how to interact with it. Together, they are critical for designing intuitive and user-friendly interfaces.

## Key Concepts
- Mental Model → User's internal understanding of a system.
- Affordance → Perceived action possibilities of an object.
- Signifier → Cues that communicate affordances.
- System Image → How the system is presented to the user.
- User Experience (UX) → Overall experience of a person using a product.
- Usability → Ease of use and learnability of a system.
