## Quick Definition
Foundational Concepts in Robotics and Automation cover the essential theoretical and practical knowledge underpinning robotic systems. This includes kinematics, dynamics, control, sensing, and actuation, providing the basis for understanding how robots move, interact with their environment, and perform tasks autonomously.

## Key Concepts
- Kinematics → Study of robot motion without considering forces.
- Dynamics → Analysis of forces and torques causing robot motion.
- Control Systems → Algorithms regulating robot behavior for desired performance.
- Sensors → Devices for perceiving the environment and gathering data.
- Actuators → Components converting signals into physical motion.
- Artificial Intelligence → Enables autonomous perception, reasoning, and learning in robots.
