## Quick Definition
Fairness and bias in AI address the critical challenge of ensuring that artificial intelligence systems operate equitably and do not perpetuate or amplify societal prejudices. It involves identifying, measuring, and actively mitigating discriminatory outcomes in AI applications.

## Key Concepts
- Algorithmic Bias → systematic errors leading to unfair outcomes.
- Fairness Metrics → quantitative tools to evaluate model impartiality.
- Disparate Impact → disproportionate negative effect on specific groups.
- Group Fairness → equal treatment or performance across demographic segments.
- Individual Fairness → similar treatment for similar individuals.
- Debiasing Techniques → methods to reduce or eliminate algorithmic prejudice.
- Explainable AI (XAI) → making AI decisions transparent to uncover bias.
- Data Bias → inherent prejudices within training datasets.
