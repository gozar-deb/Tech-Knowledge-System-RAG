## Quick Definition
Drug Discovery Informatics is an interdisciplinary field leveraging computational and data science to streamline the identification, design, and optimization of new drug candidates. It integrates cheminformatics, bioinformatics, and AI to analyze complex biological and chemical data, accelerating the development of novel therapeutics.

## Key Concepts
- Target Identification → pinpointing disease-relevant biological molecules for drug intervention
- Lead Discovery → finding initial chemical compounds with desired activity against a target
- Lead Optimization → enhancing drug candidate properties like potency, selectivity, and safety
- Virtual Screening → computational methods to filter large compound libraries for potential hits
- ADMET Prediction → forecasting drug absorption, distribution, metabolism, excretion, and toxicity
- Molecular Docking → simulating molecular interactions to predict binding modes and affinities
