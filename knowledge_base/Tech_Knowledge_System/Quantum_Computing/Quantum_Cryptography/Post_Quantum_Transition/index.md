## Quick Definition
Post-quantum transition is the strategic shift from current cryptographic standards to new algorithms resilient against quantum computer attacks. This proactive migration ensures the long-term security of digital communications and stored data, mitigating the existential threat posed by future quantum capabilities.

## Key Concepts
- Post-Quantum Cryptography (PQC) → Cryptography secure against quantum attacks
- NIST Standardization → Global effort to select and standardize PQC algorithms
- Cryptographic Agility → System's ability to switch crypto algorithms easily
- Hybrid Mode → Using classical and PQC algorithms concurrently during transition
- Quantum Threat → Risk of quantum computers breaking current encryption
- Lattice-based Cryptography → PQC family based on hard lattice problems
- Hash-based Signatures → Digital signatures using cryptographic hash functions
- Code-based Cryptography → PQC derived from error-correcting codes
