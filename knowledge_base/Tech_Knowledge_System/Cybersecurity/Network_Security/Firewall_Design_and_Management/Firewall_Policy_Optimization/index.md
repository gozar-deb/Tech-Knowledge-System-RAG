## Quick Definition
Firewall policy optimization is the continuous process of refining and organizing firewall rules to enhance network security, improve performance, and ensure compliance. It involves identifying and eliminating redundant, shadowed, or overly permissive rules, and transitioning to more granular, application-based controls.

## Key Concepts
- Rule Redundancy → Multiple rules achieving the same outcome, causing inefficiency.
- Shadowed Rules → Rules that are never activated due to preceding, broader rules.
- Overly Permissive Rules → Policies granting excessive access, increasing security risks.
- Rule Consolidation → Merging related rules to simplify and improve policy efficiency.
- Application-Based Policies → Security rules defined by application identity rather than network ports.
- Policy Lifecycle Management → The comprehensive process of managing firewall policies from creation to deprecation.
- Compliance Auditing → Regular checks to ensure firewall policies meet regulatory and internal standards.
- Traffic Flow Analysis → Examination of network traffic patterns to inform and validate policy decisions.
