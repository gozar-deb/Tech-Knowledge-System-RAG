## Quick Definition
The Future of Work explores the profound transformations in employment, labor markets, and organizational dynamics driven by technological innovation, demographic shifts, and evolving societal values. It critically examines the ethical, social, and economic implications of these changes on human labor.

## Key Concepts
- Automation → use of technology to perform tasks, impacting job roles and efficiency
- Gig Economy → flexible, short-term contract work often via digital platforms
- Human-AI Collaboration → synergistic partnership between humans and AI systems
- Reskilling/Upskilling → acquiring new or advanced skills for evolving job markets
- Algorithmic Management → data-driven oversight of workers and tasks by algorithms
- Digital Ethics → moral principles guiding technology's role in the workplace
