## Quick Definition
Signal fundamentals involve the study of basic signal types, their mathematical representations, and inherent properties. This foundational knowledge is essential for understanding how information is encoded, transmitted, and processed in various engineering and scientific disciplines.

## Key Concepts
- Analog Signal → Continuous in time and amplitude.
- Digital Signal → Discrete in time and amplitude, sampled and quantized.
- Time Domain → Signal amplitude plotted against time.
- Frequency Domain → Signal components plotted against frequency.
- Sampling → Converting continuous to discrete time.
- Quantization → Converting continuous to discrete amplitude.
- Bandwidth → Range of frequencies a signal occupies.
- Noise → Unwanted disturbances corrupting a signal.
