## Quick Definition
Behavior planning is the crucial layer in autonomous vehicles responsible for high-level decision-making, translating environmental understanding and mission objectives into safe, efficient, and socially compliant maneuvers. It orchestrates the vehicle's actions by considering traffic rules, predicted behaviors of other road users, and dynamic driving conditions.

## Key Concepts
- Decision-Making → Selecting appropriate actions (e.g., lane change, brake) based on context.
- Prediction → Forecasting movements and intentions of other road users.
- Rule-Based Systems → Governing behavior according to traffic laws and safety protocols.
- Cost Functions → Evaluating potential actions based on safety, comfort, and efficiency.
- State Machines → Modeling vehicle states and transitions for structured behavior.
- Social Compliance → Ensuring behaviors are predictable and acceptable to human drivers.
- Risk Assessment → Continuously evaluating hazards to make robust and safe choices.
