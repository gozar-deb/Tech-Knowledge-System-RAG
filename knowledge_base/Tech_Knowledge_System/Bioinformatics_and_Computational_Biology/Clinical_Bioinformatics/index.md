## Quick Definition
Clinical bioinformatics is the application of computational techniques to biological and clinical data to improve patient care. It focuses on integrating diverse omics data with clinical information for enhanced disease diagnosis, prognosis, and personalized treatment strategies.

## Key Concepts
- Variant Interpretation → Assessing the clinical impact of genetic alterations.
- Precision Medicine → Tailoring healthcare to individual patient variability.
- Pharmacogenomics → Understanding genetic influence on drug response.
- Diagnostic Informatics → Computational support for disease identification.
- Biomarker Discovery → Finding molecular indicators for health or disease.
- Clinical Decision Support → Systems aiding medical professionals with data-driven insights.
