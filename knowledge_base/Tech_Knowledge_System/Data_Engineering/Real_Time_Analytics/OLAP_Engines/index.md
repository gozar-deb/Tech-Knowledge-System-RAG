## Quick Definition
OLAP engines are specialized software systems enabling rapid, interactive, and multidimensional analysis of large datasets. They are crucial for business intelligence, allowing users to explore complex data from various perspectives to uncover insights and support decision-making.

## Key Concepts
- Data Cube → A multidimensional data structure for fast analysis.
- Dimensions → Attributes used to categorize and filter data.
- Measures → Numerical values subject to analysis.
- Roll-up → Aggregating data to higher levels of summary.
- Drill-down → Navigating from summary to detailed data.
- Slice and Dice → Filtering and subsetting data cubes for specific views.
