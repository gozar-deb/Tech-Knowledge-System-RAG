## Quick Definition
UX Research and Methods involves systematically studying users to understand their needs, behaviors, and motivations, thereby informing the design of user-centered products. It employs various techniques to gather insights, validate design decisions, and continuously improve the overall user experience.

## Key Concepts
- Usability Testing → Directly observing users interact with a product to identify issues.
- User Interviews → Engaging in direct conversations with users to uncover their perspectives and needs.
- Persona Development → Creating fictional representations of target users based on research data.
- Journey Mapping → Visualizing the steps a user takes to achieve a goal, including their emotions and pain points.
- Card Sorting → A method for understanding how users categorize information to inform information architecture.
- A/B Testing → Comparing two versions of a design element to see which performs better with users.
