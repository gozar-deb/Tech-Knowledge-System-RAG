## Quick Definition
Human-AI collaboration is the synergistic partnership between humans and artificial intelligence systems, where each leverages the other's strengths to achieve common goals. This collaboration enhances human capabilities, improves decision-making, and optimizes task execution across various domains.

## Key Concepts
- Augmented Intelligence → AI systems designed to enhance human abilities.
- Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) → Human oversight and intervention in AI processes.
- Shared Autonomy → Dynamic distribution of control between human and AI.
- Explainable AI (XAI) → AI systems providing transparent reasoning for their actions.
- Trust and Transparency → Foundational elements for effective human-AI partnerships.
- Job Redesign → Adapting roles and tasks to integrate AI tools effectively.
- Skill Augmentation → Developing new human skills for interacting with AI.
- Ethical AI Frameworks → Guidelines for responsible and fair AI deployment.
