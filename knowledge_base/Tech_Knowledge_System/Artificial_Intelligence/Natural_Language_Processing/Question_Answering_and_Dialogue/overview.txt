[CHUNK: DEFINITION]
Question Answering (QA) and Dialogue systems enable machines to understand and respond to human language queries, often in a conversational context. QA focuses on extracting precise answers from text, while dialogue systems manage multi-turn interactions to achieve user goals. Both rely heavily on NLP, machine learning, and knowledge representation techniques.

[CHUNK: CORE_CONCEPTS]
- Machine Comprehension: The ability of an AI system to read and understand text, often measured by its performance on reading comprehension tasks.
- Information Retrieval: The process of finding relevant documents or passages from a large corpus that contain potential answers to a query.
- Semantic Parsing: Converting natural language utterances into structured, machine-understandable representations, such as logical forms or SQL queries.
- Intent Recognition: Identifying the user's goal or purpose behind an utterance in a dialogue system.
- Slot Filling: Extracting specific pieces of information (entities) from a user's utterance that are required to fulfill their intent.
- Response Generation: Creating natural language responses, either by selecting pre-defined templates (retrieval-based) or synthesizing new text (generative-based).
- Context Tracking: Maintaining a memory of the conversation history to inform subsequent turns and ensure coherence.
- Coreference Resolution: Identifying when different expressions in a text refer to the same entity, crucial for understanding pronouns and repeated nouns.
- Knowledge Graphs: Structured representations of facts and relationships used to provide factual answers and enhance reasoning capabilities.
- Conversational AI: An umbrella term encompassing technologies that allow humans to interact with computers using natural language, including chatbots and virtual assistants.

[CHUNK: SYSTEM_DESIGN_PERSPECTIVE]
QA and Dialogue systems are typically modular, integrating components for natural language understanding (NLU), dialogue management (DM), and natural language generation (NLG). NLU processes user input, DM orchestrates the conversation flow and state, and NLG formulates responses. Architectures range from rule-based to end-to-end neural models, often incorporating hybrid approaches for robustness.

[CHUNK: REAL_WORLD_USE]
Google Assistant and Amazon Alexa utilize advanced dialogue systems for voice commands, information retrieval, and task completion. Customer service chatbots on banking or e-commerce websites handle routine inquiries and guide users through processes. Legal tech platforms employ QA to rapidly search and summarize relevant case law from vast document repositories.
