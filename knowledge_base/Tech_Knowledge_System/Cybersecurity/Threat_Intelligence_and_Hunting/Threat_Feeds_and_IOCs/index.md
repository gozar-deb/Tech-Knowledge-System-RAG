## Quick Definition
Threat feeds are structured streams of cyber threat intelligence, primarily composed of Indicators of Compromise (IOCs). IOCs are digital artifacts or pieces of data that indicate a potential security breach or malicious activity, enabling organizations to detect, prevent, and respond to cyber threats more effectively.

## Key Concepts
- Threat Intelligence → Actionable information about adversaries and their methods.
- Indicators of Compromise (IOCs) → Observable artifacts suggesting a system compromise.
- Threat Feeds → Automated delivery of IOCs and threat data.
- STIX/TAXII → Standards for sharing structured threat information.
- MISP → Open-source platform for threat intelligence sharing.
- Contextualization → Adding meaning and relevance to raw threat data.
