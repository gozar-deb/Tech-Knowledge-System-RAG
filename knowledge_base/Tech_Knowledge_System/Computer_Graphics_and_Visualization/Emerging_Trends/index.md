## Quick Definition
Emerging trends in computer graphics and visualization represent the forefront of innovation in digital visual content creation and interaction. These advancements, driven by AI, real-time processing, and immersive technologies, are pushing the boundaries of realism, interactivity, and automation across various industries.

## Key Concepts
- Real-Time Rendering → Instantaneous image generation for interactive applications.
- AI-Driven Automation → Machine learning optimizing graphics workflows and content creation.
- Augmented Reality (AR) → Overlaying digital elements onto the real world for enhanced experiences.
- Virtual Reality (VR) → Immersive simulated environments for highly interactive visual engagement.
- Physically Based Rendering (PBR) → Realistic simulation of light and material interactions.
- Generative AI (GANs) → AI models autonomously creating 3D assets and animations.
- Hyperrealism → Achieving photorealistic visuals that blur digital and physical boundaries.
- Interactive Visualization → Dynamic manipulation and exploration of visual data and 3D scenes.
