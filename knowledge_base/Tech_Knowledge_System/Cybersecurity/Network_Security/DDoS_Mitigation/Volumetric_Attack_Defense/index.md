## Quick Definition
Volumetric attack defense involves strategies and technologies designed to mitigate Distributed Denial of Service (DDoS) attacks that aim to overwhelm a target's network bandwidth or resources with massive volumes of traffic. These defenses focus on absorbing, filtering, and redirecting malicious traffic to maintain service availability.

## Key Concepts
- **Volumetric DDoS Attack** → Overwhelming a target with high-volume traffic to consume bandwidth.
- **Traffic Scrubbing** → Diverting traffic through specialized centers to filter out malicious packets.
- **Anomaly Detection** → Identifying unusual traffic patterns indicative of an attack.
- **Rate Limiting** → Restricting the number of requests a server accepts from a specific source.
- **BGP Anycast** → Routing traffic to the nearest clean pipe or scrubbing center for distributed defense.
- **DNS Amplification** → A common volumetric attack technique exploiting open DNS resolvers.
