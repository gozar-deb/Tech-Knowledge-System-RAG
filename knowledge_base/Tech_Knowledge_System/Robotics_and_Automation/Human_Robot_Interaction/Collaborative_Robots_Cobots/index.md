## Quick Definition
Collaborative robots, or cobots, are advanced robotic systems designed to work safely and interactively alongside humans in shared workspaces. They are characterized by inherent safety features like force and speed limiting, enabling direct human-robot collaboration without traditional safety cages, thereby enhancing flexibility and efficiency in various applications.

## Key Concepts
- **Human-Robot Interaction (HRI)** → The study of interactions between humans and robots.
- **Safety Standards (ISO 10218, ISO/TS 15066)** → International guidelines ensuring safe robot operation and collaboration.
- **Force/Torque Sensors** → Devices that measure forces and torques, crucial for safety and delicate manipulation.
- **End-Effectors** → Tools attached to the robot arm's end, such as grippers or welders, for specific tasks.
- **Teach Pendant** → A handheld device used to program and control robot movements.
- **Vision Systems** → Cameras and software enabling robots to perceive their environment and objects.
