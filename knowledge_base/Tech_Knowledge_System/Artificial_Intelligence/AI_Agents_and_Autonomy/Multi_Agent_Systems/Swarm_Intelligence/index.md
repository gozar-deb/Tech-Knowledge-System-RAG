## Quick Definition
Swarm Intelligence is an artificial intelligence paradigm inspired by the collective behavior of social insects and animal societies. It leverages decentralized, self-organized systems of simple agents to solve complex problems through local interactions and emergent global patterns.

## Key Concepts
- Decentralization → No central control, distributed decision-making.
- Self-organization → Global order arises from local interactions.
- Emergence → Complex behavior from simple rules.
- Stigmergy → Indirect communication via environmental modification.
- Particle Swarm Optimization (PSO) → Population-based optimization inspired by bird flocking.
- Ant Colony Optimization (ACO) → Metaheuristic inspired by ants finding shortest paths.
