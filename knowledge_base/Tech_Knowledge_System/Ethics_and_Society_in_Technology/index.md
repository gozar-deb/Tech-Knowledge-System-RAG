## Quick Definition
Ethics and Society in Technology is an interdisciplinary field exploring the moral, social, and political implications of technological advancements. It aims to guide responsible innovation, ensuring technology aligns with human values, promotes justice, and respects fundamental rights within a rapidly evolving digital landscape.

## Key Concepts
- Algorithmic Bias → Unfair discrimination by algorithms due to skewed data or design.
- Data Privacy → Individual control over personal information collection and use.
- Responsible Innovation → Proactive design and development of technology for societal good.
- Digital Rights → Fundamental human rights applied to the digital realm.
- Technology Governance → Policies and structures guiding technology's development and use.
- Ethical AI → Designing, developing, and deploying AI systems that adhere to moral principles.

## Tools

| Tool | Type | Purpose |
|------|------|---------|
| Ethical Impact Assessments (EIAs) | Methodology | Proactively identify and mitigate ethical risks of technology |
