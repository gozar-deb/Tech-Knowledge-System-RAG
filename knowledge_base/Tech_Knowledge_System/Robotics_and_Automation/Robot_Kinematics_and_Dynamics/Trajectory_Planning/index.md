## Quick Definition
Trajectory planning is the process of generating a time-parameterized path for a robot, specifying its position, velocity, and acceleration over time. It ensures smooth, efficient, and collision-free movement while adhering to kinematic and dynamic constraints.

## Key Concepts
- Path Generation → Creating a geometric sequence of points or poses.
- Time Parameterization → Assigning a time value to each point on the path.
- Kinematic Constraints → Limitations on joint angles, velocities, and accelerations.
- Dynamic Constraints → Restrictions based on robot mass, inertia, and motor capabilities.
- Collision Avoidance → Preventing physical contact with obstacles or other robots.
- Optimization Criteria → Metrics like minimum time, energy, or jerk to improve trajectory quality.
