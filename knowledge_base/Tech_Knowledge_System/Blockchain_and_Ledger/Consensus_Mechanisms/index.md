## Quick Definition
Consensus mechanisms are protocols that enable distributed systems, like blockchains, to agree on a single, valid state of data. They ensure all participants maintain a consistent and immutable ledger, even when faced with failures or malicious attempts to alter information.

## Key Concepts
- Proof-of-Work (PoW) → Cryptographic puzzle solving to validate blocks and secure the network.
- Proof-of-Stake (PoS) → Validators are chosen based on their staked cryptocurrency, securing the network economically.
- Byzantine Fault Tolerance (BFT) → Ability of a distributed system to reach consensus despite faulty or malicious nodes.
- Finality → The irreversible confirmation of transactions on a distributed ledger.
- Decentralization → Distribution of power and decision-making across a network, removing central control.
- Sybil Attack Resistance → Mechanisms preventing a single entity from dominating a network through multiple identities.
