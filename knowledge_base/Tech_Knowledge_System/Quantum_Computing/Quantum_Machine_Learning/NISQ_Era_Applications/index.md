## Quick Definition
NISQ Era Applications in Quantum Machine Learning leverage current noisy, intermediate-scale quantum computers for machine learning tasks. These applications often employ hybrid quantum-classical algorithms to explore potential quantum advantages in areas like optimization and classification, despite hardware limitations.

## Key Concepts
- NISQ (Noisy Intermediate-Scale Quantum) → Current quantum computing era with limited qubits and significant noise.
- Variational Quantum Algorithms (VQAs) → Hybrid algorithms optimizing quantum circuits with classical optimizers.
- Quantum Neural Networks (QNNs) → Quantum circuits mimicking classical neural networks for ML tasks.
- Quantum Feature Maps → Methods for encoding classical data into quantum states.
- Barren Plateaus → Problem in VQAs where gradients vanish, hindering optimization.
- Quantum Advantage → Demonstrating a quantum computer can outperform classical computers for a specific problem.
