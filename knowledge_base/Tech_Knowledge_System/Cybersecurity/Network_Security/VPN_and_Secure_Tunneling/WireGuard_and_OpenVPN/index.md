## Quick Definition
WireGuard and OpenVPN are leading VPN protocols and software solutions that establish secure, encrypted tunnels over untrusted networks. WireGuard is known for its modern design, simplicity, and high performance, while OpenVPN is recognized for its robust features, flexibility, and broad compatibility across various platforms.

## Key Concepts
- WireGuard: A modern, high-performance VPN protocol using state-of-the-art cryptography.
- OpenVPN: A mature, highly configurable VPN solution supporting various encryption and authentication methods.
- VPN Tunneling: Creating a secure, private connection over a public network.
- Cryptographic Primitives: Underlying algorithms (e.g., ChaCha20-Poly1305, AES-256) for encryption and authentication.
- Public Key Cryptography: Used for secure key exchange and peer authentication in WireGuard.
- TLS/SSL: Utilized by OpenVPN for secure communication and certificate-based authentication.
