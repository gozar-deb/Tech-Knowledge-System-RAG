## Quick Definition
Single-cell omics is a suite of high-resolution molecular techniques that analyze individual cells rather than bulk populations. It reveals cellular heterogeneity, dynamic states, and rare cell types by profiling genomics, transcriptomics, proteomics, and metabolomics at an unprecedented level of detail.

## Key Concepts
- Cellular Heterogeneity → Variation among individual cells within a population.
- Multi-omics Integration → Combining different molecular data types (e.g., DNA, RNA, protein) from single cells.
- Spatial Transcriptomics → Measuring gene expression while retaining spatial information within tissues.
- Data Sparsity → The challenge of many zero values in single-cell expression matrices.
- Batch Effects → Technical variations introduced by different experimental runs or conditions.
- Cell Trajectory Inference → Reconstructing developmental or disease progression pathways from single-cell data.
