## Quick Definition
RNA-Seq Analysis is a powerful next-generation sequencing technique that quantifies and characterizes RNA transcripts in a biological sample. It provides insights into gene expression levels, alternative splicing events, and novel transcripts, crucial for understanding cellular function and disease mechanisms.

## Key Concepts
- Transcriptome → The full collection of RNA molecules in a cell or organism.
- Gene Expression Quantification → Measuring the activity level of genes by counting RNA transcripts.
- Differential Expression Analysis → Identifying genes with statistically significant expression changes between conditions.
- Read Alignment → Mapping short sequencing reads to a reference genome or transcriptome.
- Alternative Splicing → Producing multiple protein isoforms from a single gene through differential exon usage.
- Gene Fusion → A hybrid gene formed by the combination of two distinct genes, often associated with cancer.
