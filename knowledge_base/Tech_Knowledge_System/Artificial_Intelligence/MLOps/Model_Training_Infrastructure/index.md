## Quick Definition
Model Training Infrastructure in MLOps provides the robust and scalable environment necessary for developing, training, and validating machine learning models efficiently. It automates resource allocation, manages experiments, and ensures reproducibility across various stages of the ML lifecycle, bridging the gap between research and production.

## Key Concepts
- MLOps → Practices for deploying and maintaining ML models in production reliably and efficiently.
- Scalability → Ability to handle increasing data and model complexity with flexible resource allocation.
- Reproducibility → Ensuring consistent training outcomes by tracking all components: code, data, and environment.
- Experiment Tracking → Logging and comparing different model training runs, metrics, and artifacts.
- Resource Orchestration → Automated management and scheduling of computational resources for training jobs.
- CI/CD for ML → Adapting continuous integration/delivery principles to ML pipelines for automated testing and deployment.
