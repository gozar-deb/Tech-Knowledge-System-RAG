## Quick Definition
Protein structure prediction is the computational process of determining a protein's three-dimensional shape from its amino acid sequence. This is essential for understanding biological function, disease mechanisms, and for applications in biotechnology and drug development.

## Key Concepts
- Homology Modeling → Predicts structure using known related protein structures as templates.
- Ab Initio Prediction → Builds protein structure from scratch, relying only on sequence information and physical principles.
- Protein Threading → Aligns a protein sequence to a library of known protein folds to find the best structural match.
- Molecular Dynamics → Simulates atomic interactions over time to observe protein movement and conformational changes.
- Contact Prediction → Uses computational methods, often deep learning, to identify residues that are close in 3D space.
- Force Fields → Mathematical functions and parameters used in simulations to describe the potential energy of a system.
