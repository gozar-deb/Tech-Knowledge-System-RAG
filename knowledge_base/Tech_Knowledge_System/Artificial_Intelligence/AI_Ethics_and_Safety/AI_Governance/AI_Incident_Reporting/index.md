## Quick Definition
AI Incident Reporting is the structured process of documenting, analyzing, and communicating failures or adverse events involving artificial intelligence systems. Its purpose is to facilitate learning, improve AI safety, and ensure accountability by systematically addressing system malfunctions and their impacts.

## Key Concepts
- Incident Classification → Categorizing AI failures by type, impact, and severity.
- Root Cause Analysis → Investigating underlying factors contributing to an AI incident.
- Post-Mortem Analysis → Detailed review after an incident to learn lessons and improve systems.
- Reporting Frameworks → Structured guidelines and templates for documenting AI incidents.
- Transparency and Disclosure → Communicating incident details to relevant stakeholders.
- Remediation Strategies → Actions taken to fix issues and prevent recurrence of AI incidents.
