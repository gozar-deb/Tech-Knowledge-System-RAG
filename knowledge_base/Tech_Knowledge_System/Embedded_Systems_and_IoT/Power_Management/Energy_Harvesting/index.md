## Quick Definition
Energy harvesting is the process of converting ambient energy from the environment into usable electrical power to enable self-powered electronic devices. It extends device lifespan and reduces reliance on traditional batteries, crucial for remote or maintenance-free applications.

## Key Concepts
- Transduction → Converting environmental energy into electrical energy.
- Ambient Energy Sources → Environmental forms like light, heat, motion, RF, and vibrations.
- Low Power Electronics → Devices optimized for minimal power consumption.
- Power Management Units (PMUs) → Circuits managing harvested energy storage and delivery.
- Energy Storage → Components like supercapacitors or batteries for power buffering.
- Self-Powered Devices → Autonomous systems running solely on harvested energy.
- Hybrid Harvesting → Combining multiple energy sources or conversion methods.
