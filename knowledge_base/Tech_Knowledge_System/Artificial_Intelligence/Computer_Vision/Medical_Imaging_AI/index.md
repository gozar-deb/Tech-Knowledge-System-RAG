## Quick Definition
Medical Imaging AI leverages artificial intelligence, primarily deep learning and computer vision, to analyze medical images for enhanced diagnosis, prognosis, and treatment planning. It automates complex image interpretation tasks, improving efficiency and accuracy in healthcare.

## Key Concepts
- Image Segmentation → Precisely delineating structures or anomalies in images.
- Object Detection → Locating and identifying specific features like lesions or organs.
- Image Classification → Categorizing images based on medical conditions or characteristics.
- Radiomics → Extracting quantitative data from images for predictive analytics.
- Explainable AI (XAI) → Ensuring AI decisions are transparent and understandable to clinicians.
- Data Augmentation → Artificially expanding datasets to improve model robustness.
- Transfer Learning → Applying knowledge from pre-trained models to new medical tasks.
- Multi-modal Fusion → Integrating data from various sources for comprehensive analysis.
