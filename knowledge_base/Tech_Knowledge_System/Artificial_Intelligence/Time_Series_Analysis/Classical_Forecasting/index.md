## Quick Definition
Classical forecasting encompasses statistical techniques that analyze historical time-ordered data to predict future values. These methods identify underlying patterns like trends and seasonality, providing interpretable models for various predictive tasks. They form the bedrock of traditional time series analysis.

## Key Concepts
- Stationarity → Time series properties remain constant over time.
- Trend → Long-term upward or downward movement in data.
- Seasonality → Regular, repeating patterns over fixed periods.
- Autocorrelation → Correlation of a time series with its past values.
- ARIMA → A statistical model combining autoregression, differencing, and moving average.
- Exponential Smoothing → Models that assign decreasing weights to older observations.
