## Quick Definition
Human-Robot Interaction (HRI) is an interdisciplinary field focused on the design, study, and implementation of interfaces and interactions between humans and robots. It aims to create natural, safe, and effective communication and collaboration, optimizing both human experience and robot performance.

## Key Concepts
- Shared Autonomy → cooperative control blending human and robot capabilities
- Robot Trust → human's belief in robot reliability and competence
- Social Robotics → designing robots for socially appropriate human interaction
- Human Factors → considering human capabilities and psychology in robot design
- Robot Ethics → addressing moral and societal implications of robotic systems
- Multimodal Interaction → using various communication channels (voice, gesture, touch)
