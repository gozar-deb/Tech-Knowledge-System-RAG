## Quick Definition
Graph Neural Networks (GNNs) for Molecular Science apply deep learning to molecular structures, treating them as graphs to predict properties, facilitate drug discovery, and design new materials. This field leverages the inherent graph-like nature of molecules for advanced computational analysis and accelerates scientific discovery.

## Key Concepts
- Molecular Graphs → Representation of molecules as nodes (atoms) and edges (bonds).
- Message Passing → Iterative information exchange between nodes in a GNN.
- Molecular Property Prediction → Forecasting chemical/physical properties of molecules.
- De Novo Molecular Design → Generating novel molecules with desired characteristics.
- Graph Convolutions → Generalizing CNNs to graph-structured molecular data.
- Equivariant GNNs → Models that respect molecular symmetries and 3D geometry.
