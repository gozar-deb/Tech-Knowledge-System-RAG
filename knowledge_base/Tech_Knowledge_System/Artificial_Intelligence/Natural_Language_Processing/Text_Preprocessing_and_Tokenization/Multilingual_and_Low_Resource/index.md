## Quick Definition
This field focuses on adapting text preprocessing and tokenization techniques for languages with diverse linguistic structures and limited digital resources. It addresses challenges like morphological complexity, varied writing systems, and data scarcity to enable effective NLP for a global linguistic landscape.

## Key Concepts
- Subword Tokenization → Breaks words into smaller units to handle OOV words and morphology.
- Language Identification → Automatically detects the language of a given text.
- Unicode Normalization → Standardizes character representations across different languages.
- Data Augmentation → Artificially expands training data for low-resource languages.
- Cross-Lingual Transfer Learning → Applies knowledge from high-resource to low-resource languages.
- Morphological Analysis → Examines word internal structure, crucial for inflected languages.
