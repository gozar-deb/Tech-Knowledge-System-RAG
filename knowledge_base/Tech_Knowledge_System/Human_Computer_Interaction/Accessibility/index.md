## Quick Definition
Accessibility in Human-Computer Interaction (HCI) focuses on designing and developing digital products and environments that are usable by people with diverse abilities and disabilities. It aims to remove barriers, ensuring equitable access and participation for all users, regardless of their sensory, cognitive, or motor capabilities.

## Key Concepts
- Inclusive Design → Designing for the full spectrum of human diversity.
- Assistive Technology (AT) → Tools and software aiding individuals with disabilities.
- Universal Design → Creating universally usable products without special adaptations.
- WCAG → International guidelines for web content accessibility.
- Screen Readers → Software that vocalizes on-screen text for visually impaired users.
- Keyboard Navigation → Interacting with interfaces solely using a keyboard.
- Cognitive Accessibility → Designing for clarity and simplicity for users with cognitive differences.
