## Quick Definition
GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation) is a comprehensive EU law on data protection and privacy for all individuals within the European Union and the European Economic Area. NDPR (Nigeria Data Protection Regulation) is a similar framework in Nigeria, establishing principles and requirements for processing personal data. Both aim to grant individuals greater control over their personal data and impose strict obligations on organizations handling such data.

## Key Concepts
- Data Subject Rights → Rights of individuals regarding their personal data (e.g., access, rectification, erasure).
- Data Controller → Entity determining the purposes and means of processing personal data.
- Data Processor → Entity processing personal data on behalf of a controller.
- Lawful Basis for Processing → Legal grounds required to process personal data (e.g., consent, contract, legitimate interest).
- Data Protection Impact Assessment (DPIA) → Process to identify and minimize data protection risks of a project.
- Cross-Border Data Transfer → Rules governing the transfer of personal data outside its originating jurisdiction.
- Data Protection Officer (DPO) → Designated individual responsible for overseeing data protection strategy and compliance.
