## Quick Definition
AI governance is the comprehensive framework of policies, processes, and standards designed to ensure the responsible, ethical, and compliant development and deployment of artificial intelligence systems. It aims to mitigate risks, uphold societal values, and build public trust in AI technologies.

## Key Concepts
- Ethical AI → Guiding principles for morally sound AI development.
- Responsible AI → Practices for accountable, fair, and transparent AI systems.
- AI Regulation → Legal frameworks governing AI to ensure public welfare.
- AI Frameworks → Structured approaches for implementing governance within organizations.
- Risk Management → Processes to identify and mitigate AI-related harms and biases.
- Compliance → Adherence to legal and ethical standards for AI systems.
- Transparency → Understanding AI decision-making processes.
- Accountability → Assigning responsibility for AI system outcomes.
- Fairness → Ensuring equitable treatment by AI systems.
- Explainability → Interpreting and communicating AI model reasoning.
