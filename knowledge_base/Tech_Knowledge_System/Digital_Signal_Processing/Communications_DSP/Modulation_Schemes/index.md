## Quick Definition
Modulation schemes are essential techniques in communication systems that involve encoding information onto a carrier wave by varying its properties (amplitude, frequency, or phase). This process enables efficient and reliable transmission of data over various channels, forming the backbone of modern digital and analog communications.

## Key Concepts
- Amplitude Shift Keying (ASK) → Data encoded by carrier amplitude variations
- Frequency Shift Keying (FSK) → Data encoded by carrier frequency variations
- Phase Shift Keying (PSK) → Data encoded by carrier phase shifts
- Quadrature Amplitude Modulation (QAM) → Combines amplitude and phase modulation for higher data rates
- Analog Modulation → Continuous variation of carrier properties (e.g., AM, FM)
- Demodulation → Process of recovering original information from modulated signal
- Spectral Efficiency → Measure of how efficiently bandwidth is utilized
