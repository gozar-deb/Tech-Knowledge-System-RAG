## Quick Definition
Tensor algebra is a mathematical framework that generalizes linear algebra to multilinear maps, enabling the representation and manipulation of higher-order data structures. It extends concepts of scalars, vectors, and matrices to tensors of arbitrary rank, crucial for fields like machine learning and theoretical physics.

## Key Concepts
- Tensor → A multilinear map generalizing scalars, vectors, and matrices.
- Rank (Order) → Number of indices defining a tensor's components.
- Tensor Product → Operation combining tensors to form a higher-rank tensor.
- Contraction → Operation reducing tensor rank by summing over paired indices.
- Covariant/Contravariant Indices → Lower/upper indices indicating vector space or dual space elements.
- Metric Tensor → Defines inner products and index manipulation in curved spaces.
