## Quick Definition
Multi-omics integration is the computational and statistical process of combining data from multiple biological omics layers (e.g., genomics, transcriptomics, proteomics, metabolomics) to gain a more comprehensive understanding of biological systems. This approach aims to uncover complex interactions and regulatory mechanisms that are not apparent from single-omics analyses, providing deeper insights into disease mechanisms and biological processes.

## Key Concepts
- Genomics → Study of an organism's complete set of DNA, including genes and their interactions.
- Transcriptomics → Analysis of RNA transcripts, their sequences, and expression levels within a cell or organism.
- Proteomics → Large-scale study of proteins, their structures, functions, and interactions in biological systems.
- Metabolomics → Systematic study of chemical processes involving metabolites, the small molecules in cells, tissues, or organisms.
- Data Integration → The process of combining diverse datasets from different omics platforms to form a unified view.
- Systems Biology → A holistic approach to understanding biological systems by studying the interactions between their components.
- Network Analysis → Computational methods to model and analyze relationships and interactions within biological data, often represented as graphs.
- Machine Learning → Algorithms applied to multi-omics data for pattern recognition, prediction, and classification tasks.
