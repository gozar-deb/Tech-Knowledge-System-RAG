## Quick Definition
Cirq is Google's open-source Python framework for developing and running quantum algorithms on NISQ devices. OpenQASM is a low-level, machine-independent assembly language used to describe quantum circuits for execution on various quantum hardware platforms.

## Key Concepts
- Quantum Circuit: A series of quantum operations applied to qubits.
- Qubit: The fundamental unit of quantum information.
- Quantum Gate: An operation that transforms qubit states.
- NISQ: Noisy Intermediate-Scale Quantum, referring to current quantum hardware.
- Quantum Assembly Language: A low-level language for quantum instructions.
- Interoperability: The ability of different systems (frameworks, hardware) to work together, often via OpenQASM.
