## Quick Definition
AI Transparency refers to the clarity and understandability of an AI system's internal workings and decision-making processes. It aims to make AI models, data, and algorithmic logic intelligible to humans, fostering trust, enabling accountability, and facilitating ethical deployment.

## Key Concepts
- Explainable AI (XAI) → Methods to make AI decisions understandable to humans.
- Interpretability → The extent to which one can comprehend the cause of a model's output.
- Accountability → Responsibility for AI system outcomes and impacts.
- Algorithmic Transparency → Openness about algorithm design and operation.
- Data Transparency → Understanding the origin, biases, and processing of training data.
- Post-hoc Explanations → Techniques applied after a model is trained to explain its predictions.
