## Quick Definition
Environmental Impact in Technology refers to the ecological consequences of technological development and usage, encompassing resource consumption, pollution, and energy use. It evaluates the footprint of digital systems from creation to disposal, aiming for sustainable practices.

## Key Concepts
- Green Computing → Designing and using computing resources efficiently to minimize environmental harm.
- E-waste → Electronic waste, discarded electronic devices containing hazardous materials.
- Carbon Footprint → Total greenhouse gas emissions from technology-related activities.
- Lifecycle Assessment (LCA) → Comprehensive evaluation of a product's environmental impact throughout its existence.
- Circular Economy → A model of production and consumption that keeps products and materials in use for as long as possible.
- Digital Sustainability → Integrating environmental and social responsibility into digital technology.
