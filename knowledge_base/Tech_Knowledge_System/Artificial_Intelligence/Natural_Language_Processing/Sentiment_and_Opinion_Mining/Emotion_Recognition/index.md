## Quick Definition
Emotion Recognition is an advanced area within AI and NLP focused on computationally identifying and categorizing human emotions from various data sources. It goes beyond basic positive/negative sentiment to detect specific emotional states, crucial for understanding nuanced human communication and behavior.

## Key Concepts
- Affective Computing → The broader field encompassing the study and development of systems that can recognize, interpret, process, and simulate human affects.
- Valence-Arousal-Dominance (VAD) Model → A common dimensional model describing emotions along three continuous axes: pleasure (valence), activation (arousal), and control (dominance).
- Emotional Lexicons → Dictionaries of words annotated with emotional scores or categories, used in rule-based or hybrid systems.
- Contextual Embeddings → Vector representations of words or phrases that capture their meaning based on surrounding text, vital for nuanced emotion detection.
- Multimodal Fusion → Techniques for combining features from different modalities (e.g., text, audio, video) to improve emotion recognition accuracy.
- Emotion Cause Extraction → Identifying the specific events or reasons that trigger a particular emotion in text.
