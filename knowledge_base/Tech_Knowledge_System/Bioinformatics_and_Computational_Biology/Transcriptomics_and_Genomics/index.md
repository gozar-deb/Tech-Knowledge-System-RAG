## Quick Definition
Transcriptomics is the comprehensive study of RNA transcripts, while genomics investigates the entire DNA genome. Both fields utilize high-throughput sequencing to analyze gene expression, genetic variation, and genome structure, providing deep insights into biological processes and disease mechanisms.

## Key Concepts
- RNA-Seq → Quantifies gene expression by sequencing RNA.
- DNA Sequencing → Determines the nucleotide order in DNA.
- Gene Expression → Process of converting genetic information into functional products.
- Genome Assembly → Reconstructing a complete genome from fragments.
- Variant Calling → Identifying genetic differences from a reference genome.
- Epigenetics → Heritable changes in gene function not involving DNA sequence changes.
