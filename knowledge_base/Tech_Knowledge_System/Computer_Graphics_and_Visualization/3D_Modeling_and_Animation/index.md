## Quick Definition
3D modeling is the digital creation of three-dimensional object representations, while 3D animation brings these models to life through movement over time. These core computer graphics disciplines are essential for visual media, interactive experiences, and digital simulations.

## Key Concepts
- Polygonal Modeling → Building objects from vertices, edges, and faces.
- NURBS → Smooth, mathematically precise curves and surfaces.
- Rigging → Creating a digital skeleton for model manipulation.
- Skinning → Attaching mesh to bones for realistic deformation.
- Keyframe Animation → Defining poses at specific times for interpolated motion.
- Motion Capture → Recording real-world movement for 3D character animation.
- Texturing → Applying visual details and material properties to surfaces.
- Rendering → Generating final 2D images from 3D scenes.
