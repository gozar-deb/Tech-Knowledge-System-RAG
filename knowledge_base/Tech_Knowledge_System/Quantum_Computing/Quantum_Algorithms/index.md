## Quick Definition
Quantum algorithms are specialized computational methods designed for quantum computers, exploiting quantum mechanics to achieve computational advantages over classical algorithms for certain problems. They promise exponential speedups in areas like cryptography, search, and simulation, driving the next era of computing.

## Key Concepts
- Qubit → The fundamental unit of quantum information, existing in superposition.
- Superposition → A state where a qubit can be 0, 1, or both simultaneously.
- Entanglement → A quantum phenomenon linking qubits, where their states are correlated.
- Quantum Gates → Basic operations that transform qubit states within a quantum circuit.
- Shor's Algorithm → A quantum algorithm for efficient integer factorization.
- Grover's Algorithm → A quantum algorithm for quadratically faster unstructured database search.
- Quantum Annealing → A quantum optimization method for finding global minima.
- Variational Quantum Eigensolver (VQE) → A hybrid algorithm for chemical simulations.
- Quantum Approximate Optimization Algorithm (QAOA) → A hybrid algorithm for combinatorial optimization.
- Decoherence → The loss of quantum properties due to environmental interaction, a major challenge.
