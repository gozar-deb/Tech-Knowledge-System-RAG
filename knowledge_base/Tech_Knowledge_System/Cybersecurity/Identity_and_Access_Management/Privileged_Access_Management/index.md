## Quick Definition
Privileged Access Management (PAM) is a critical cybersecurity discipline focused on securing, controlling, and monitoring elevated access rights to an organization's most sensitive systems and data. It minimizes the risk of privileged credential abuse by implementing policies like least privilege and just-in-time access, ensuring accountability and compliance.

## Key Concepts
- Privileged Account → An account with elevated permissions to critical systems.
- Just-in-Time (JIT) Access → Temporary, on-demand granting of privileged access.
- Credential Vaulting → Secure storage and management of privileged credentials.
- Session Monitoring → Real-time observation and recording of privileged user activities.
- Least Privilege → Granting only the necessary permissions for a task.
- Secrets Management → Securing non-human credentials like API keys and database passwords.
