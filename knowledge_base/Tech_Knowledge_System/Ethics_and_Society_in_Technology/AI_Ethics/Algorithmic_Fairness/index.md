## Quick Definition
Algorithmic fairness ensures AI systems produce equitable outcomes, avoiding discrimination against individuals or groups. It involves identifying, measuring, and mitigating biases in data, models, and decision-making processes. The goal is to build trustworthy AI that treats all demographic segments justly.

## Key Concepts
- Bias Detection → identifying statistical disparities in data or model outputs
- Bias Mitigation → techniques to reduce or eliminate identified biases
- Fairness Metrics → quantitative measures like disparate impact or equal opportunity
- Group Fairness → ensuring similar outcomes across demographic groups
- Individual Fairness → treating similar individuals similarly, regardless of group
- Counterfactual Fairness → prediction remains same if sensitive attributes were changed
