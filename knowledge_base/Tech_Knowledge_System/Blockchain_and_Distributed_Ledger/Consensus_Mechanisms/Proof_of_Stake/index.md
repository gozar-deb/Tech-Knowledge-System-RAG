## Quick Definition
Proof of Stake (PoS) is a blockchain consensus mechanism where participants stake cryptocurrency to validate transactions and create new blocks. This method secures the network through economic incentives, penalizing malicious behavior via slashing staked assets, and offers improved energy efficiency over Proof of Work.

## Key Concepts
- Staking → Locking up tokens as collateral for validation rights.
- Validator → A node operator selected to propose and attest to blocks.
- Slashing → Economic penalty for misbehavior, resulting in loss of staked funds.
- Delegation → Allowing token holders to assign their voting power to a validator.
- Finality → The irreversible confirmation of transactions on the blockchain.
- Economic Security → The cost to corrupt the network, tied to the value of staked assets.
