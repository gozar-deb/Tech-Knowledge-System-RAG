## Quick Definition
Foundational Concepts in Bioinformatics and Computational Biology encompass the core theoretical principles and methodologies that underpin the analysis of biological data using computational approaches. This field integrates computer science, statistics, and molecular biology to interpret complex biological information, driving discoveries in genomics, proteomics, and systems biology.

## Key Concepts
- Bioinformatics → The application of computational techniques to manage and analyze biological information.
- Computational Biology → The development and application of data-analytical and theoretical methods, mathematical modeling and computational simulation techniques to the study of biological, behavioral, and social systems.
- Sequence Analysis → Methods for analyzing DNA, RNA, and protein sequences to identify genes, regulatory elements, and evolutionary relationships.
- Structural Biology → Computational methods for predicting and analyzing the 3D structures of biological macromolecules.
- Genomics → The study of entire genomes, including gene mapping, sequencing, and analysis of gene function.
- Proteomics → The large-scale study of proteins, particularly their structures and functions.
- Algorithms → Computational procedures used to solve specific problems, such as sequence alignment or phylogenetic tree construction.
- Data Mining → Techniques for discovering patterns and insights from large biological datasets.
- Machine Learning → Application of AI algorithms to learn from biological data and make predictions or classifications.
- Systems Biology → An approach to understand biological systems by studying the interactions between their components.
