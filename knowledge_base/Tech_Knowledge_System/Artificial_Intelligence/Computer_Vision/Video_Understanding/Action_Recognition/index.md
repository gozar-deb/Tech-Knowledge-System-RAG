## Quick Definition
Action Recognition is a subfield of computer vision focused on identifying and classifying human actions or activities within video sequences. It analyzes spatio-temporal patterns to understand dynamic behaviors, crucial for applications ranging from surveillance to human-computer interaction.

## Key Concepts
- Spatial-Temporal Features → Information from both visual appearance and motion over time.
- Human Pose Estimation → Tracking key body points to understand movement.
- Activity Classification → Assigning labels to observed action sequences.
- Temporal Modeling → Capturing sequential dependencies of actions over time.
- Action Proposal Generation → Identifying video segments likely containing actions.
- Fine-grained Recognition → Distinguishing subtle variations of similar actions.
