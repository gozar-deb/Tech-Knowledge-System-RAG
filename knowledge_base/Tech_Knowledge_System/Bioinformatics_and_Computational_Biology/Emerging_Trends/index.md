## Quick Definition
Emerging trends in bioinformatics and computational biology refer to the cutting-edge technologies, methodologies, and conceptual frameworks that are currently gaining prominence and are expected to significantly impact the future of biological research and applications. These trends often involve interdisciplinary approaches, leveraging advancements in AI, data science, and molecular biology to address complex biological problems.

## Key Concepts
- **Artificial Intelligence in Biology** → Application of AI and machine learning algorithms to analyze complex biological data, predict molecular interactions, and accelerate drug discovery.
- **Single-cell Omics** → Technologies that allow for the study of genomics, transcriptomics, proteomics, and metabolomics at the resolution of individual cells, revealing cellular heterogeneity.
- **Spatial Transcriptomics** → Methods that enable the measurement of gene expression while retaining spatial information within tissues, providing insights into cellular organization and function.
- **Long-read Sequencing** → Next-generation sequencing technologies that produce reads thousands to millions of base pairs long, improving genome assembly, structural variant detection, and isoform identification.
- **Personalized Medicine** → An approach to medical treatment that tailors healthcare decisions, treatments, practices, and products to the individual patient based on their genetic makeup, environment, and lifestyle.
- **Synthetic Biology** → The design and construction of new biological parts, devices, and systems, or the redesign of existing natural biological systems for useful purposes.
- **Quantum Computing in Bioinformatics** → Exploration of quantum algorithms to solve computationally intensive problems in bioinformatics, such as protein folding, drug design, and large-scale genomic analysis.
