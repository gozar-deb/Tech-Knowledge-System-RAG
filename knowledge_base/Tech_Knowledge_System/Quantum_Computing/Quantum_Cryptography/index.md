## Quick Definition
Quantum Cryptography is a field that uses the principles of quantum mechanics to create secure communication methods, primarily for key exchange. It ensures that any attempt by an eavesdropper to intercept the communication is detectable, providing unconditional security.

## Key Concepts
- Quantum Key Distribution (QKD) → Method for secure key exchange using quantum mechanics.
- Heisenberg Uncertainty Principle → Fundamental principle making eavesdropping detectable.
- Quantum Entanglement → Phenomenon used in some QKD protocols for key generation.
- No-Cloning Theorem → Prevents perfect copying of quantum states, crucial for security.
- BB84 Protocol → The first and most widely known QKD protocol.
- Photon Polarization → How quantum information is often encoded in QKD systems.
