## Quick Definition
Anomaly Detection in Time Series is the process of identifying data points, events, or observations that deviate significantly from the normal behavior of a time-ordered sequence. It is crucial for maintaining system health, detecting fraud, and ensuring data integrity by highlighting unexpected patterns.

## Key Concepts
- **Outlier Detection** → Identifying individual data points that are abnormal.
- **Change Point Detection** → Locating specific moments where the statistical properties of a time series change.
- **Novelty Detection** → Identifying new, unseen patterns that were not present in the training data.
- **Forecasting-based Methods** → Using predictive models to forecast future values and flagging deviations from these forecasts as anomalies.
- **Statistical Process Control** → Applying statistical methods to monitor and control a process, detecting anomalies when measurements fall outside control limits.
- **Pattern Recognition** → Identifying unusual sequences or subsequences within the time series data.
- **Unsupervised Learning** → Detecting anomalies without labeled data, relying on inherent data structure.
- **Supervised Learning** → Training models on labeled anomalous and normal data for classification.
