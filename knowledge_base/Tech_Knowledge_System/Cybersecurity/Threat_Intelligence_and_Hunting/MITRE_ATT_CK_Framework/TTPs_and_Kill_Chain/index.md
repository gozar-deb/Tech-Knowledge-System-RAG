## Quick Definition
TTPs (Tactics, Techniques, and Procedures) detail how adversaries execute cyberattacks, while the Cyber Kill Chain models the sequential stages of an attack. These frameworks provide a structured approach to understanding, analyzing, and defending against cyber threats by categorizing adversary actions and attack progression.

## Key Concepts
- Tactics → High-level adversary objectives (e.g., Initial Access).
- Techniques → Specific methods to achieve tactics (e.g., Spearphishing).
- Procedures → Detailed steps or tools used for techniques.
- Cyber Kill Chain → Seven-stage model of an attack lifecycle.
- MITRE ATT&CK → Knowledge base of adversary TTPs.
- Threat Hunting → Proactive search for undetected threats using TTPs.
