## Quick Definition
AI Ethics is a critical field dedicated to understanding and addressing the moral, social, and legal implications of artificial intelligence. It provides principles and frameworks to ensure AI systems are developed and deployed responsibly, promoting fairness, transparency, and accountability while mitigating potential harms.

## Key Concepts
- Algorithmic Bias → Systematic errors in AI leading to unfair outcomes.
- Fairness → Equitable treatment of individuals and groups by AI systems.
- Transparency → Understanding AI's decision-making processes and data usage.
- Accountability → Assigning responsibility for AI actions and impacts.
- Privacy → Protecting personal data and autonomy in AI contexts.
- Human-Centered AI → Designing AI to augment human capabilities and values.
