## Quick Definition
Quantum computing is a novel computational paradigm that harnesses quantum-mechanical phenomena like superposition and entanglement to process information. It enables the solution of certain complex problems exponentially faster than classical computers, opening new frontiers in science, medicine, and technology.

## Key Concepts
- Qubit → The fundamental unit of quantum information, existing in multiple states simultaneously.
- Superposition → A quantum state where a qubit can be 0, 1, or both at the same time.
- Entanglement → A strong correlation between qubits, where their states are linked regardless of distance.
- Quantum Gates → Operations that manipulate qubit states, analogous to classical logic gates.
- Quantum Algorithm → A set of quantum operations designed to exploit quantum properties for computation.
- Decoherence → The loss of quantum properties due to interaction with the environment, a major challenge.
