## Quick Definition
The Incident Response (IR) Lifecycle is a systematic framework for managing cybersecurity incidents, encompassing preparation, detection, analysis, containment, eradication, recovery, and post-incident activities. Its primary goal is to minimize damage, restore operations, and enhance an organization's security posture against future threats.

## Key Concepts
- Preparation → Proactive measures like policy development, training, and tool deployment.
- Detection & Analysis → Identifying security events and understanding their nature and scope.
- Containment → Limiting the spread and impact of an active security incident.
- Eradication → Removing the root cause of the compromise from affected systems.
- Recovery → Restoring systems and data to a secure, operational state.
- Post-Incident Activity → Reviewing lessons learned and improving processes after an incident.
- Playbooks → Standardized procedures for handling specific incident types.
- Forensics → Investigating digital evidence to understand incident details.
