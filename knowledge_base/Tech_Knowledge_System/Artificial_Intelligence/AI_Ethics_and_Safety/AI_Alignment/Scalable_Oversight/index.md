## Quick Definition
Scalable oversight is a critical area in AI alignment research focused on developing methods for humans to effectively supervise and control AI systems, even as those systems become more intelligent and capable than their human creators. It addresses the challenge of ensuring advanced AI remains beneficial and aligned with human values.

## Key Concepts
- Human-in-the-loop → Human involvement in AI decision-making and evaluation.
- Superhuman AI → AI systems exceeding human intellectual capacity.
- Weak-to-strong generalization → Using weaker AI to supervise stronger AI.
- AI interpretability → Understanding AI's internal reasoning and decisions.
- Reward modeling → Training AI to learn and act on human preferences.
- AI safety → Ensuring AI systems operate without causing harm or unintended consequences.
