## Quick Definition
Probability and Statistics provide the mathematical tools to understand, quantify, and manage uncertainty. They are fundamental for data analysis, predictive modeling, and decision-making in computational systems, forming the bedrock for fields like machine learning and artificial intelligence.

## Key Concepts
- Probability Distributions → Describe the likelihood of different outcomes for random variables.
- Statistical Inference → Process of drawing conclusions about a population from sample data.
- Hypothesis Testing → A method for making decisions about a population based on sample data.
- Regression Analysis → Statistical process for estimating the relationships among variables.
- Bayesian Statistics → A framework for statistical inference where probability expresses a degree of belief.
- Markov Chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) → Algorithms for sampling from a probability distribution.
- Central Limit Theorem → States that the distribution of sample means approximates a normal distribution.
- Overfitting → A modeling error where a function is too closely aligned to a limited set of data points.
