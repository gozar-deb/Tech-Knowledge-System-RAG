## Quick Definition
The Carbon Footprint of AI quantifies the greenhouse gas emissions generated by artificial intelligence systems across their entire lifecycle. This includes the energy consumed during model training and inference, as well as the environmental cost of manufacturing the underlying hardware.

## Key Concepts
- Operational Carbon → Emissions from electricity used during training and inference.
- Embodied Carbon → Emissions from manufacturing and disposing of AI hardware.
- Power Usage Effectiveness (PUE) → Metric for data center energy efficiency.
- Carbon Intensity → Emissions produced per unit of electricity consumed by the grid.
- Green AI → Practices aimed at making AI computationally and environmentally efficient.
- Scope 3 Emissions → Indirect value chain emissions, including cloud infrastructure.
