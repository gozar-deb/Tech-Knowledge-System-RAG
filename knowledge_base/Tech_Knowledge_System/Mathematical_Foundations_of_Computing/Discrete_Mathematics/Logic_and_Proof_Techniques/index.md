## Quick Definition
Logic and Proof Techniques are the bedrock of rigorous reasoning in mathematics and computer science, providing systematic methods to construct valid arguments and establish the truth of statements. They encompass formal systems for propositional and predicate logic, alongside various proof strategies like induction and contradiction, essential for verifying correctness and consistency in complex systems.

## Key Concepts
- Propositional Logic → Deals with truth values of simple and compound statements.
- Predicate Logic → Extends propositional logic with quantifiers for complex assertions.
- Direct Proof → Proving a conclusion directly from premises.
- Proof by Contradiction → Assuming the opposite and deriving a contradiction.
- Mathematical Induction → Proving statements for all natural numbers.
- Inference Rules → Principles for deriving new truths from existing ones.
- Formal Verification → Using mathematical techniques to prove system correctness.
