## Quick Definition
Privacy and data rights define the legal and ethical principles governing how personal information is collected, processed, and shared. They empower individuals with control over their data, ensuring protection against unauthorized access and misuse in the digital realm.

## Key Concepts
- Personal Data → Information identifying an individual.
- Data Subject → The individual whose data is processed.
- Data Controller → Entity deciding data processing purposes.
- Data Processor → Entity processing data on controller's behalf.
- Consent → Explicit permission for data processing.
- Right to be Forgotten → Right to have personal data erased.
- Data Minimization → Principle of collecting only essential data.
- Privacy by Design → Embedding privacy into system architecture.
