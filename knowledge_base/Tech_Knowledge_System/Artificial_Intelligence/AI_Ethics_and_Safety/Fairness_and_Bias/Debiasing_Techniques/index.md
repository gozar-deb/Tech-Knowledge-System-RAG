## Quick Definition
Debiasing techniques are methods employed in AI systems to reduce or eliminate unfair biases stemming from data, algorithms, or system design. Their goal is to ensure equitable outcomes and prevent discriminatory impacts across diverse demographic groups, fostering responsible and trustworthy AI.

## Key Concepts
- Algorithmic Fairness → Principle of AI systems operating without prejudice or discrimination.
- Bias Mitigation → Strategies to reduce or remove unwanted biases in AI.
- Pre-processing Debiasing → Techniques applied to training data before model training.
- In-processing Debiasing → Methods integrated into the model training process.
- Post-processing Debiasing → Adjustments made to model predictions after training.
- Fairness Metrics → Quantitative measures like demographic parity or equalized odds to assess fairness.
- Disparate Impact → Practices disproportionately affecting one group, even unintentionally.
- Counterfactual Fairness → Outcome remains same if sensitive attributes were changed.
