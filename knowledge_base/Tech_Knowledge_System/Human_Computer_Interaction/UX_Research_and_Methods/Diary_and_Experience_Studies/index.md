## Quick Definition
Diary studies and experience sampling methods (ESM) are qualitative UX research techniques that capture user behaviors, thoughts, and feelings over time in their natural environments. They provide rich, longitudinal data essential for understanding real-world user interactions and informing design decisions.

## Key Concepts
- Diary Study → Participants self-report experiences over an extended period.
- Experience Sampling Method (ESM) → Real-time prompts for immediate user feedback.
- Longitudinal Research → Studies observing changes and patterns over time.
- In-Context Data → Information gathered from users in their natural settings.
- Self-Reporting → Users actively document their own interactions and feelings.
- Qualitative Data → Non-numerical insights into user motivations and behaviors.
- Ecological Validity → Research findings' applicability to real-world scenarios.
- User Journey Mapping → Visualizing the complete user experience over time.
