## Quick Definition
Voice and Conversational User Interfaces (VUI/CUI) enable users to interact with systems using natural language, either spoken or typed. They leverage AI technologies like speech recognition and natural language understanding to interpret user intent and respond intelligently, creating intuitive and accessible digital experiences.

## Key Concepts
- Speech Recognition → Converts spoken words into text for processing.
- Natural Language Understanding (NLU) → Interprets the meaning and intent of user input.
- Dialogue Management → Controls the flow and context of a conversation.
- Natural Language Generation (NLG) → Creates human-like text responses.
- Text-to-Speech (TTS) → Synthesizes spoken audio from text.
- Intent Recognition → Identifies the user's goal from their utterance.
