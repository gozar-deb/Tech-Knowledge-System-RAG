## Quick Definition
Complexity classes are fundamental categories in theoretical computer science that group computational problems based on the amount of resources (time and space) required to solve them. They provide a framework for understanding the inherent difficulty of problems and the limits of efficient computation.

## Key Concepts
- P → Problems solvable in polynomial time by a deterministic algorithm.
- NP → Problems whose solutions can be verified in polynomial time.
- NP-complete → The hardest problems in NP, to which all other NP problems can be reduced.
- NP-hard → Problems at least as hard as NP-complete problems.
- PSPACE → Problems solvable using a polynomial amount of memory.
- EXPTIME → Problems solvable in exponential time.
- L → Problems solvable in logarithmic space.
- NL → Problems solvable in logarithmic space by a non-deterministic algorithm.
- coNP → Problems whose complements are in NP.
