## Quick Definition
Pathology and Histology AI leverages artificial intelligence, particularly deep learning, to analyze digital pathology images for automated disease detection, grading, and quantification. It aims to enhance diagnostic accuracy and efficiency in medical pathology by processing high-resolution tissue scans.

## Key Concepts
- Whole Slide Imaging (WSI) → Digital scanning of glass slides for computational analysis.
- Image Segmentation → Isolating specific structures like cells or tumor regions.
- Deep Learning → Neural networks for complex pattern recognition in images.
- Disease Classification → Categorizing tissue samples based on AI analysis.
- Prognostic Modeling → Predicting patient outcomes from histological features.
- Explainable AI (XAI) → Making AI decisions transparent and understandable for pathologists.
