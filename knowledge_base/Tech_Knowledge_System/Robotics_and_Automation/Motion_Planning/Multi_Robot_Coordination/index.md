## Quick Definition
Multi-robot coordination focuses on enabling multiple autonomous robots to work together effectively to achieve shared or individual objectives. It involves complex algorithms for task distribution, communication, and conflict resolution to maximize collective performance and robustness.

## Key Concepts
- Decentralized Control → robots make local decisions without a central authority
- Task Allocation → assigning specific jobs to individual robots
- Collision Avoidance → preventing robots from colliding with each other or obstacles
- Communication Protocols → rules for robots to exchange information
- Formation Control → maintaining desired spatial arrangements among robots
- Swarm Intelligence → emergent collective behavior from simple individual interactions
- Resource Sharing → managing access to common assets or tools
- Consensus Algorithms → achieving agreement among robots on a shared state or decision
