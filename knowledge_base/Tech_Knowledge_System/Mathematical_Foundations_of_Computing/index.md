## Quick Definition
Mathematical Foundations of Computing is the theoretical bedrock of computer science, encompassing discrete mathematics, logic, and computability theory. It provides the essential tools and rigorous frameworks for designing, analyzing, and understanding computational systems and algorithms.

## Key Concepts
- Discrete Mathematics → Study of countable structures, vital for algorithms.
- Logic → Formal reasoning for program correctness and AI.
- Set Theory → Basis for data modeling and abstract structures.
- Graph Theory → Modeling relationships in networks and data.
- Computability Theory → What problems can be solved by computers.
- Complexity Theory → Resources needed to solve computational problems.
- Formal Languages → Defining programming language syntax.
- Automata Theory → Abstract machines and their computational power.
