## Quick Definition
Cybersecurity ethics explores the moral principles governing the use and development of cybersecurity technologies. It addresses the ethical dilemmas arising from data protection, privacy, and digital security measures, aiming to balance security needs with individual rights and societal values.

## Key Concepts
- Privacy-by-Design → Integrating privacy considerations into system architecture from the outset.
- Ethical Hacking → Authorized security testing to find vulnerabilities, adhering to strict ethical codes.
- Data Sovereignty → The concept that data is subject to the laws and governance structures of the nation where it is collected.
- Algorithmic Bias → Unfair or discriminatory outcomes produced by algorithms, often due to biased training data.
- Responsible Disclosure → The ethical practice of reporting security vulnerabilities to vendors before public release.
- Digital Human Rights → The application of fundamental human rights to the digital sphere, including freedom of expression and access.
