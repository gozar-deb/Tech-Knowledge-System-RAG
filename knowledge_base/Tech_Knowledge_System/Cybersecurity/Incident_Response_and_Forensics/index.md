## Quick Definition
Incident Response and Forensics (IRF) is a specialized cybersecurity domain that encompasses the structured processes for managing and investigating security breaches. It focuses on minimizing damage, recovering systems, and analyzing digital evidence to understand attack methodologies and prevent future occurrences.

## Key Concepts
- Incident Response Lifecycle → Phases from preparation to post-incident analysis.
- Digital Forensics → Scientific methods for collecting, preserving, and analyzing digital evidence.
- Chain of Custody → Documented process ensuring integrity and admissibility of evidence.
- Malware Analysis → Examination of malicious software to understand its behavior and impact.
- Threat Intelligence Integration → Using external data to enrich incident detection and response.
- Containment Strategies → Techniques to limit the scope and impact of an ongoing incident.
