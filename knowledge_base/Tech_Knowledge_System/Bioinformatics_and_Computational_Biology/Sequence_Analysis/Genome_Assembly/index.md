## Quick Definition
Genome assembly is the process of computationally reconstructing a complete genome sequence from fragmented DNA reads. It involves aligning and merging these short sequences to determine their original order and orientation, ultimately aiming to recreate the entire chromosomal structure of an organism.

## Key Concepts
- Reads → Short DNA fragments generated by sequencing technologies.
- De Novo Assembly → Assembling a genome without a reference sequence.
- Contigs → Contiguous DNA sequences formed by overlapping reads.
- Scaffolds → Ordered and oriented contigs separated by gaps of known size.
- Repeats → Genomic regions that occur multiple times, complicating assembly.
- Assemblers → Software algorithms used to perform genome reconstruction.
- Hybrid Assembly → Combining short and long reads for improved assembly quality.
