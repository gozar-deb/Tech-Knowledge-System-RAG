## Quick Definition
Threat Intelligence and Hunting is a proactive cybersecurity discipline focused on gathering and analyzing threat data to identify and neutralize unknown or advanced threats. It combines contextualized information about adversaries with iterative searches through systems to detect malicious activities that bypass traditional defenses.

## Key Concepts
- Threat Intelligence → Knowledge about threats, including IOCs and TTPs.
- Threat Hunting → Proactive search for undetected threats within a network.
- IOCs → Data points indicating potential malicious activity.
- TTPs → Adversary behaviors and methods for achieving objectives.
- MITRE ATT&CK → Framework mapping adversary tactics and techniques.
- Cyber Kill Chain → Model describing stages of a cyberattack.
- SIEM → System for centralized security log management and analysis.
- EDR → Endpoint monitoring and response solutions.
