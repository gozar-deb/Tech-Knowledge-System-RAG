## Quick Definition
Toil in SRE is the manual, repetitive, and automatable operational work that scales with system growth but offers no lasting value. Its reduction is crucial for freeing up SREs to focus on strategic engineering and system improvements.

## Key Concepts
- Toil Characteristics → Manual, repetitive, automatable, tactical, no enduring value.
- Toil Measurement → Quantifying time spent on operational overhead.
- Automation → Core strategy to eliminate manual, repetitive tasks.
- Error Budgets → Indirectly drives toil reduction by prioritizing reliability work.
- Service Level Objectives (SLOs) → Define desired service reliability, influencing where toil reduction is needed.
- SRE Time Allocation → Balancing toil with strategic engineering work.
