## Quick Definition
Sentiment and Opinion Mining, a key area in NLP, involves using computational methods to identify and extract subjective information from text, determining the emotional tone or attitude expressed towards specific subjects. It classifies text into categories like positive, negative, or neutral, providing insights into public sentiment.

## Key Concepts
- Polarity Classification → Assigning positive, negative, or neutral labels to text.
- Aspect-Based Sentiment Analysis (ABSA) → Determining sentiment towards specific entities or features.
- Subjectivity Detection → Differentiating factual statements from opinions.
- Sentiment Lexicons → Curated lists of words with associated sentiment scores.
- Emotion Recognition → Identifying specific human emotions like joy, anger, or sadness.
- Transfer Learning → Adapting pre-trained language models for sentiment tasks.
