## Quick Definition
AI Ethics and Safety is the discipline dedicated to ensuring the responsible development, deployment, and governance of artificial intelligence. It focuses on mitigating risks like bias and privacy violations, promoting fairness, transparency, and accountability, and aligning AI systems with human values and societal well-being.

## Key Concepts
- Fairness → Ensuring equitable treatment and outcomes from AI systems.
- Transparency → Understanding AI decision-making processes and internal workings.
- Accountability → Assigning responsibility for AI system actions and impacts.
- Data Privacy → Protecting sensitive information within AI data pipelines.
- Reliability → Guaranteeing consistent and predictable AI system performance.
- Human Oversight → Maintaining human control and intervention capabilities over AI.
