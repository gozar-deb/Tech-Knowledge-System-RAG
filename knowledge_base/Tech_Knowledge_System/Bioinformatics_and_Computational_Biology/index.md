## Quick Definition
Bioinformatics is the application of computational techniques to manage and analyze large biological datasets, such as DNA sequences and protein structures. Computational biology focuses on developing theoretical models and algorithms to simulate and understand complex biological systems and processes.

## Key Concepts
- Sequence Alignment → Comparing biological sequences to find regions of similarity, indicating functional or evolutionary relationships.
- Phylogenetic Trees → Graphical representations showing evolutionary relationships among species or genes.
- Gene Expression Analysis → Quantifying gene activity to understand biological processes and disease states.
- Protein Structure Prediction → Determining the three-dimensional shape of a protein from its amino acid sequence.
- Systems Biology → Studying biological systems as integrated networks of genes, proteins, and metabolites.
- Big Data in Biology → Managing and analyzing extremely large and complex biological datasets.
