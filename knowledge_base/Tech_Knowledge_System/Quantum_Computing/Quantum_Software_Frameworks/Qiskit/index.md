## Quick Definition
Qiskit is an open-source software development kit (SDK) for programming quantum computers, developed by IBM. It provides a comprehensive suite of tools for building, optimizing, and executing quantum circuits and algorithms on both quantum simulators and real quantum hardware.

## Key Concepts
- Quantum Circuit: A sequence of quantum operations applied to qubits.
- Qubit: The basic unit of quantum information, representing a quantum bit.
- Quantum Gate: A basic quantum operation that transforms the state of qubits.
- Quantum Algorithm: A set of instructions designed to run on a quantum computer to solve a computational problem.
- Quantum Hardware: Physical devices that perform quantum computations.
- Quantum Simulation: Emulating quantum computer behavior on classical computers.
