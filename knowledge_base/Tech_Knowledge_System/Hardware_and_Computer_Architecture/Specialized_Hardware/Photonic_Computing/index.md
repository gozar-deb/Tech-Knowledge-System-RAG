## Quick Definition
Photonic computing harnesses light (photons) instead of electricity (electrons) for data processing and transmission. This approach offers significant advantages in speed, energy efficiency, and parallelism, addressing limitations of conventional electronic computing.

## Key Concepts
- Photons as Information Carriers → Utilizes light particles for data processing and communication.
- Optical Integrated Circuits (PICs) → Miniaturized photonic components on a single chip for complex optical functions.
- Parallel Processing → Enables simultaneous computations due leveraging light's non-interacting nature.
- Energy Efficiency → Reduces heat generation and power consumption compared to electronic systems.
- High-Speed Data Transfer → Facilitates ultra-fast communication due to light's inherent speed.
- Neuromorphic Photonic Systems → Mimics brain structures for AI and machine learning applications.
