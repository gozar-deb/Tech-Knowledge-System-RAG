## Quick Definition
Data Quality and Governance is the strategic discipline focused on ensuring data assets are accurate, consistent, and reliable throughout their lifecycle. It involves establishing policies, processes, and responsibilities to manage data effectively, supporting informed decision-making and regulatory compliance.

## Key Concepts
- Data Integrity → Maintaining accuracy and consistency of data.
- Data Lineage → Tracking data's journey from source to consumption.
- Master Data Management (MDM) → Creating a single, trusted view of core business data.
- Metadata Management → Managing information about data for better understanding.
- Data Stewardship → Assigning accountability for data assets.
- Data Catalog → A searchable inventory of an organization's data assets.
