## Quick Definition
AR Overlay Principles are the core guidelines for integrating digital information and virtual objects onto the real world in augmented reality experiences. They focus on ensuring seamless blending, intuitive interaction, and a coherent user perception of the augmented environment.

## Key Concepts
- Tracking → Determining the AR device\'s position and orientation in the real world.
- Registration → Precisely aligning and locking digital content to physical space.
- Latency → The delay in updating AR visuals to match user movement, impacting immersion.
- Vergence-Accommodation Conflict → Mismatch between eye focus and convergence in AR displays.
- Interaction Models → Methods for users to engage with virtual elements in AR.
- Contextual Intelligence → AR systems understanding the environment and user intent for adaptive overlays.
