## Quick Definition
Planning and Reasoning are core AI capabilities enabling agents to autonomously devise action sequences to achieve goals and infer new information. They involve computational methods for decision-making, problem-solving, and adapting to dynamic environments based on represented knowledge.

## Key Concepts
- Planning → Devising action sequences to reach a goal state.
- Reasoning → Inferring conclusions from known facts and rules.
- State-Space Search → Exploring possible states to find a solution path.
- Knowledge Representation → Structuring information for AI processing.
- Action Selection → Choosing optimal actions in a given context.
- Goal-Oriented Behavior → Agents acting purposefully towards objectives.
- Heuristics → Guiding search with informed estimates.
- Logical Inference → Deriving new truths from premises.
