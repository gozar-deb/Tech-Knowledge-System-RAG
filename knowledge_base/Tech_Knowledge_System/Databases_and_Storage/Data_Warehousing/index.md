## Quick Definition
Data warehousing is a core component of business intelligence, involving the systematic collection, integration, and storage of large volumes of historical data from various sources. It is optimized for analytical querying and reporting, providing a unified view of an organization's data to support strategic decision-making and insights.

## Key Concepts
- ETL → Extract, Transform, Load: The process of moving data from source systems to the data warehouse.
- Dimensional Modeling → A logical design technique for structuring data for analytical queries.
- Star Schema → A simple dimensional model with a central fact table and denormalized dimension tables.
- OLAP → Online Analytical Processing: Enables multidimensional analysis of business data.
- Data Mart → A subset of a data warehouse focused on a specific business function or department.
- Data Lake → A repository that stores raw data in its native format until needed, often complementing a data warehouse.
