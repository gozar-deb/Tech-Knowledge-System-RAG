## Quick Definition
The Industrial Internet of Things (IIoT) integrates smart sensors, actuators, and edge computing into industrial processes to enable advanced data collection, analysis, and automated control. It bridges the gap between Information Technology (IT) and Operational Technology (OT) to drive efficiency, predictive maintenance, and smart manufacturing.

## Key Concepts
- Cyber-Physical Systems → Integration of computation with physical processes.
- Edge Computing → Localized data processing to reduce latency and bandwidth.
- Digital Twin → Virtual replica of physical assets for simulation and monitoring.
- Predictive Maintenance → Data-driven forecasting of equipment failures.
- SCADA → Supervisory control and data acquisition for industrial processes.
- IT/OT Convergence → Integration of enterprise systems with industrial control networks.
