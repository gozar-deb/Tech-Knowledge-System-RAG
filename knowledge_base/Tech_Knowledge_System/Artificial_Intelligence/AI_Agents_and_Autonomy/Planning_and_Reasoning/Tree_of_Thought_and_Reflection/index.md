## Quick Definition
Tree of Thought (ToT) is an advanced reasoning framework for Large Language Models (LLMs) that structures problem-solving as a tree of intermediate thought steps. Coupled with reflection, it enables agents to evaluate, self-correct, and refine their reasoning paths, leading to more robust and accurate solutions for complex tasks.

## Key Concepts
- Thought States → Intermediate steps or partial solutions in a reasoning process.
- State Evaluation → Scoring thought states to guide the search for optimal solutions.
- Search Algorithms → Methods like BFS/DFS applied to explore the tree of thought states.
- Self-Correction → The agent's ability to identify and fix errors in its own reasoning.
- Deliberation → Careful consideration and evaluation before committing to an action.
- Backtracking → Reverting to a previous state when a current path is unproductive.
- Prompt Engineering → Designing effective prompts to guide LLM thought generation and reflection.
- Cognitive Architectures → The structural design of an intelligent system's mental processes.
