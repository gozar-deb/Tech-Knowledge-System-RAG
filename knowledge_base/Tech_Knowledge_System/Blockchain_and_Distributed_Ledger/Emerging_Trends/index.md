## Quick Definition
Emerging trends in blockchain and distributed ledger technology (DLT) represent the cutting edge of innovation, addressing scalability, privacy, and interoperability challenges. These advancements are shaping the future of decentralized applications, digital ownership, and autonomous organizations, pushing the boundaries of what DLT can achieve.

## Key Concepts
- Web3 → The decentralized internet, empowering users with ownership and control.
- DeFi → Blockchain-based financial services, aiming for open and permissionless systems.
- NFTs → Unique digital assets for verifiable ownership of digital or physical items.
- DAOs → Community-governed organizations operating via smart contracts.
- Interoperability → Seamless communication and asset transfer between different blockchains.
- Zero-Knowledge Proofs → Cryptographic technique for privacy-preserving verification.
