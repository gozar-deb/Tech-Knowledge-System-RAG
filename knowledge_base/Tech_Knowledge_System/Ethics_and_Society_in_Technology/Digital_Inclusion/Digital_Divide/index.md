## Quick Definition
The digital divide signifies the global and local disparities in access to, use of, and impact from information and communication technologies. It's a complex issue driven by socio-economic, geographic, and educational factors, leading to unequal opportunities in the digital age.

## Key Concepts
- Digital Access → Availability of internet connectivity and devices.
- Digital Literacy → Skills required to effectively use digital technologies.
- Digital Equity → Fair distribution of digital resources and opportunities.
- Infrastructure Gap → Lack of adequate broadband and hardware in certain regions.
- Socio-economic Disparity → Income and wealth differences impacting technology adoption.
- Policy Interventions → Government and organizational efforts to bridge the divide.
