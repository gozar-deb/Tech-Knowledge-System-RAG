## Quick Definition
Data observability provides comprehensive visibility into the health, quality, and reliability of data across its lifecycle. It enables proactive identification and resolution of data issues, ensuring data integrity and trust for downstream applications and analytics.

## Key Concepts
- Data Monitoring → Continuous tracking of data metrics for anomalies and deviations.
- Data Quality → Ensuring accuracy, completeness, consistency, validity, and uniqueness of data.
- Data Reliability → Guaranteeing data is available, consistent, and performs as expected.
- Data Lineage → Tracking data's origin, transformations, and movement across systems.
- Anomaly Detection → Automated identification of unusual patterns or outliers in data.
- Incident Management → Processes for detecting, triaging, and resolving data-related issues.
- Data Health → Overall state of data quality, reliability, and availability.
