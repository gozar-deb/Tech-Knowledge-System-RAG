## Quick Definition
Chaos Engineering is the practice of intentionally introducing failures into a system to identify weaknesses and build confidence in its resilience. It's a proactive approach to validate system robustness against turbulent conditions in production environments.

## Key Concepts
- Fault Injection → Deliberately introducing errors or failures.
- Steady State → The normal, expected behavior of a system.
- Hypothesis → A prediction about system behavior under failure.
- Blast Radius → The potential impact area of a chaos experiment.
- Game Days → Scheduled simulations of real-world outages.
- Observability → Understanding system internals from external data.
