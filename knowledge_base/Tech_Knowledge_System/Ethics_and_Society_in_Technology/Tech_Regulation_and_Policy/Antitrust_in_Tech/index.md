## Quick Definition
Antitrust in Tech involves applying competition laws to the technology sector to prevent monopolies and anti-competitive practices. Its goal is to ensure fair market dynamics, foster innovation, and protect consumer interests in the rapidly evolving digital economy.

## Key Concepts
- Antitrust Laws → Regulations preventing monopolies and promoting fair competition.
- Monopoly Power → A firm's ability to control prices or exclude competition.
- Consumer Welfare Standard → Antitrust enforcement principle focusing on consumer harm.
- Anti-competitive Conduct → Business practices that restrict market competition.
- Network Effects → Product value increases with user base, potentially leading to dominance.
- Innovation Markets → Markets defined by R&D efforts for new products.
- Self-preferencing → Platforms favoring their own products/services over competitors.

## Tools

| Tool | Type | Purpose |
|------|------|---------|
| Sherman Act | Legislation | Prohibits anti-competitive agreements and monopolization |
| Clayton Act | Legislation | Addresses specific anti-competitive practices like mergers and interlocking directorates |
| Federal Trade Commission (FTC) | Regulatory Body | Enforces antitrust laws and promotes consumer protection |
| Department of Justice (DOJ) Antitrust Division | Regulatory Body | Investigates and prosecutes antitrust violations |
