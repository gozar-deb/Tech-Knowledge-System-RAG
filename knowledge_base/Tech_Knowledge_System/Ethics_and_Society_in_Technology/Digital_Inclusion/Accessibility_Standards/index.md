## Quick Definition
Accessibility standards are critical guidelines and legal requirements that ensure digital products and services are usable by everyone, including individuals with disabilities. They provide a framework for creating inclusive online experiences, promoting equal access to information and functionality.

## Key Concepts
- WCAG → International technical standards for web content accessibility.
- ADA → U.S. civil rights law extending to digital accessibility.
- Section 508 → U.S. federal law requiring accessible government IT.
- Inclusive Design → Designing for the widest possible range of human diversity.
- Assistive Technologies → Tools like screen readers that aid disabled users.
- Digital Equity → Ensuring equal access to information technology for all.
- Usability → Ease of use and effectiveness of a digital system.
- Remediation → Process of fixing accessibility issues in existing systems.
