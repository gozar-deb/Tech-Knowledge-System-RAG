## Quick Definition
Tech Regulation and Policy involves the legal and governance frameworks that dictate how technology is developed, deployed, and managed. It ensures that technological advancements align with societal values, protecting user privacy, promoting fair competition, and mitigating systemic risks.

## Key Concepts
- Data Privacy Laws → Regulations like GDPR and CCPA governing personal data handling.
- Algorithmic Transparency → Requirements for AI systems to be explainable and auditable.
- Antitrust and Competition → Policies preventing monopolistic practices in the tech industry.
- Data Localization → Mandates requiring data to be stored and processed within specific borders.
- Compliance-as-Code → Automating regulatory adherence through programmable policies.
- Digital Rights → The extension of human rights into the digital space, including freedom of expression and privacy.
