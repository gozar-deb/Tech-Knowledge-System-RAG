## Quick Definition
Combinatorics is the study of counting, arrangement, and selection of discrete objects. It provides methods for enumerating possibilities and analyzing finite structures, forming a cornerstone of discrete mathematics and computer science.

## Key Concepts
- Permutations → Ordered arrangements of objects.
- Combinations → Unordered selections of objects.
- Generating Functions → Power series for encoding sequences.
- Inclusion-Exclusion Principle → Counting elements in set unions.
- Pigeonhole Principle → Guaranteeing at least one item in a container.
- Recurrence Relations → Defining sequences based on previous terms.
- Graph Theory → Modeling relationships between objects.
- Combinatorial Design → Arranging elements into subsets with properties.
