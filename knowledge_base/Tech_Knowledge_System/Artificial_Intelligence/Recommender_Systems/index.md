## Quick Definition
Recommender systems are intelligent algorithms that predict user preferences and suggest relevant items or content. They personalize user experiences by analyzing past interactions and behavioral data, playing a critical role in various digital platforms.

## Key Concepts
- Collaborative Filtering → Recommends based on similar users or items.
- Content-Based Filtering → Suggests items similar to past preferences.
- Hybrid Recommender Systems → Combines multiple recommendation approaches.
- Matrix Factorization → Decomposes user-item interactions to find latent factors.
- Cold Start Problem → Challenge of recommending for new users or items.
- Filter Bubbles → Limited exposure to diverse content due to personalization.
- Popularity Bias → Over-recommending popular items.
- Latent Features → Hidden characteristics learned by models.
- Implicit Feedback → User actions like clicks or views.
- Explicit Feedback → Direct ratings or reviews from users.
