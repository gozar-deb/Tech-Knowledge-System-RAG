## Quick Definition
Information Architecture (IA) is the practice of organizing, structuring, and labeling content in digital environments to enhance usability and findability. It focuses on creating intuitive navigation and content hierarchies that align with user mental models.

## Key Concepts
- Organization Systems → Methods for categorizing and structuring information
- Labeling Systems → How information is represented to users
- Navigation Systems → Means by which users move through an information space
- Search Systems → Tools for querying and retrieving specific information
- Findability → Ease with which users locate desired information
- Usability → Overall ease of use and learnability of a system
- Mental Models → Users' internal representations of how a system works
- Taxonomy → Practice and science of classification for structured categorization
