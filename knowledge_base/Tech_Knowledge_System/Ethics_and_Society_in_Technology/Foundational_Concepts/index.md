## Quick Definition
Foundational Concepts in Ethics and Society in Technology are the bedrock principles and theoretical frameworks that guide the ethical analysis and responsible development of technology. They provide the essential vocabulary and analytical tools to understand and address the moral and societal implications arising from technological advancements.

## Key Concepts
- Deontology → Ethical theory focused on duties and rules.
- Consequentialism → Ethical theory judging actions by their outcomes.
- Virtue Ethics → Focuses on the moral character of the agent.
- Algorithmic Bias → Unfair discrimination by computational systems.
- Data Privacy → Control over personal information usage.
- Digital Divide → Inequality in access to technology.
- Responsible Innovation → Proactive assessment of technology impacts.
- Human-Centric Design → Prioritizing human well-being in tech.
