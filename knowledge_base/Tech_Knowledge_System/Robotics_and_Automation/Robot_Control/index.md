## Quick Definition
Robot control is the engineering discipline focused on designing and implementing the algorithms and systems that dictate a robot's actions. It integrates sensing, planning, and actuation to enable robots to perform tasks autonomously or semi-autonomously within their environment.

## Key Concepts
- Kinematics → Study of robot motion without considering forces.
- Dynamics → Study of forces and torques causing robot motion.
- Path Planning → Generating collision-free trajectories for robots.
- Motion Control → Regulating robot position, velocity, or force.
- Feedback Control → Using sensor data to correct robot behavior.
- Actuators → Components converting signals into physical movement.
