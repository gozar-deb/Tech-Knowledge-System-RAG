## Quick Definition
Low-power design techniques focus on minimizing energy consumption in embedded systems and IoT devices to extend battery life and enable operation in energy-constrained environments. This involves a combination of hardware and software optimizations, including power gating, clock gating, and dynamic voltage scaling.

## Key Concepts
- Power Gating → selectively turning off power to inactive circuit blocks.
- Clock Gating → disabling clock signals to inactive functional units.
- Dynamic Voltage and Frequency Scaling (DVFS) → adjusting voltage/frequency based on workload.
- Sleep Modes → low-power states to reduce system energy consumption.
- Energy Harvesting → capturing ambient energy to power devices.
- Power-Aware Software → optimizing code for minimal computational cycles.
