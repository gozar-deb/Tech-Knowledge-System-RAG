## Quick Definition
Quantum software frameworks are essential toolkits that enable developers to design, simulate, and execute quantum algorithms. They provide an abstraction layer over complex quantum hardware, facilitating the creation and testing of quantum programs across various platforms.

## Key Concepts
- Quantum Circuit → A series of quantum operations applied to qubits.
- Qubit → The fundamental unit of quantum information, exhibiting superposition and entanglement.
- Quantum Gate → Basic operations that transform qubit states.
- Quantum Simulator → Software emulating quantum computer behavior on classical systems.
- Hardware Abstraction → Interface allowing code portability across different quantum processors.
- Quantum Machine Learning → Applying quantum principles to machine learning tasks.
