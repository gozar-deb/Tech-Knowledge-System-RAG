## Quick Definition
Containment and Eradication are pivotal stages in incident response, focusing on limiting the impact of a security incident and completely removing the threat from affected systems. Containment isolates the threat, while Eradication eliminates its presence and prevents recurrence.

## Key Concepts
- Containment Strategies → Methods to stop an incident's spread and minimize damage.
- Eradication Procedures → Steps to remove malicious components and restore system integrity.
- Forensic Preservation → Techniques to collect evidence while performing response actions.
- Root Cause Identification → Determining the underlying vulnerability or cause of the incident.
- Incident Playbooks → Predefined steps and actions for responding to specific incident types.
- System Hardening → Implementing security controls to prevent future similar incidents.
