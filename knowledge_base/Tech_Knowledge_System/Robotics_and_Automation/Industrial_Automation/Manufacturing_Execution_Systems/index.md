## Quick Definition
Manufacturing Execution Systems (MES) are comprehensive software solutions that manage and monitor work-in-process on the factory floor in real-time. They optimize production operations by tracking materials, machines, personnel, and quality, ensuring efficient and compliant manufacturing processes.

## Key Concepts
- Production Scheduling → Optimizing manufacturing order sequences.
- Resource Management → Allocating and tracking production assets.
- Data Collection → Real-time acquisition of shop floor data.
- Quality Management → Monitoring and controlling product quality.
- Product Genealogy → Tracing product history and components.
- Performance Analysis → Evaluating production efficiency via KPIs.
