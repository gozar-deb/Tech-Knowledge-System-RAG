[CHUNK: DEFINITION]
Decentralized Finance (DeFi) refers to an ecosystem of financial applications built on blockchain technology, primarily Ethereum, that aims to disintermediate traditional financial services. It leverages smart contracts to enable peer-to-peer transactions and financial instruments without the need for central authorities like banks or brokers. DeFi promotes open, transparent, and permissionless access to financial products.

[CHUNK: CORE_CONCEPTS]
- Smart Contracts: Self-executing agreements with the terms directly written into code, forming the backbone of DeFi protocols.
- Liquidity Pools: Crowdsourced pools of cryptocurrencies locked in smart contracts, facilitating decentralized trading and lending.
- Yield Farming: The practice of staking or lending crypto assets to generate high returns or rewards in the form of additional cryptocurrency.
- Staking: Locking up cryptocurrency to support the operations of a blockchain network, earning rewards in return.
- Decentralized Exchanges (DEXs): Platforms enabling direct cryptocurrency trading between users without an intermediary.
- Lending/Borrowing Protocols: Smart contract-based systems allowing users to lend their crypto for interest or borrow against their crypto collateral.

[CHUNK: SYSTEM_DESIGN_PERSPECTIVE]
DeFi systems are architected as a stack of interconnected protocols built on a base layer blockchain, typically Ethereum. This layered design includes settlement layers (blockchain), asset layers (tokens), protocol layers (lending, DEXs), and application layers (user interfaces).
