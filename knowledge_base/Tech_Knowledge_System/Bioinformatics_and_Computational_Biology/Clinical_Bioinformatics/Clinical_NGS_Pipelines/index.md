## Quick Definition
Clinical Next-Generation Sequencing (NGS) pipelines are bioinformatics workflows that process raw genomic data to identify clinically relevant genetic variations. They are critical for diagnostic applications, enabling precision medicine through accurate and reproducible analysis of patient samples.

## Key Concepts
- NGS Data Processing → Quality control, trimming, and alignment of sequencing reads.
- Variant Calling → Detection of genetic alterations like SNVs, indels, and structural variants.
- Annotation → Linking identified variants to genomic databases and clinical significance.
- Clinical Interpretation → Assessing variant pathogenicity and relevance for diagnosis or treatment.
- Pipeline Validation → Rigorous testing for accuracy, sensitivity, and reproducibility in clinical settings.
- Quality Control (QC) → Monitoring data quality throughout the analysis workflow.
