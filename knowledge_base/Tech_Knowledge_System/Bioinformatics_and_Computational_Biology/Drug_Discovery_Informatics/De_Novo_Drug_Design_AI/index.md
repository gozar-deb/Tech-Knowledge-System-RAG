## Quick Definition
De Novo Drug Design AI employs artificial intelligence to create novel molecular structures with specific therapeutic properties from fundamental chemical principles. This computational approach accelerates drug discovery by intelligently exploring chemical space and optimizing compounds for biological targets, moving beyond traditional screening methods.

## Key Concepts
- Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs) → AI models for generating new data instances, including molecules.
- Variational Autoencoders (VAEs) → Neural networks for learning latent representations and generating new data.
- Reinforcement Learning (RL) → Training agents to make sequences of decisions to achieve a goal, applied to molecular optimization.
- Molecular Docking → Computational method predicting the preferred orientation of one molecule to a second when bound.
- Quantitative Structure-Activity Relationship (QSAR) → Predictive models linking chemical structure to biological activity.
- Synthetic Accessibility → Evaluating how easily a designed molecule can be synthesized in a laboratory.
