## Quick Definition
Automated Market Makers (AMMs) are decentralized protocols that facilitate cryptocurrency trading on blockchain networks without traditional order books. They utilize liquidity pools and mathematical algorithms to determine asset prices and enable permissionless, automatic exchanges.

## Key Concepts
- Liquidity Pool → A collection of funds locked in a smart contract, used for trading.
- Constant Product Formula → The primary algorithm (x*y=k) used by many AMMs to maintain asset ratios and determine prices.
- Impermanent Loss → Temporary loss of funds experienced by liquidity providers due to price divergence of pooled assets.
- Slippage → The difference between the expected price of a trade and the price at which the trade is executed.
- Yield Farming → The practice of staking or lending crypto assets to generate high returns or rewards.
- Decentralized Exchange (DEX) → A cryptocurrency exchange that operates without a central authority, often powered by AMMs.
