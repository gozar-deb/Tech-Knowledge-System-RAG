## Quick Definition
Foundational Concepts in Blockchain and Distributed Ledger Technology (DLT) define the core principles and architectural elements that enable secure, transparent, and tamper-resistant transaction recording and asset management in decentralized networks. These concepts include cryptography, consensus mechanisms, immutability, and decentralization, forming the bedrock for trustless systems.

## Key Concepts
- Distributed Ledger → A shared, synchronized database across multiple participants.
- Immutability → Data, once recorded, cannot be altered or deleted.
- Cryptography → Secures communications and verifies transactions through mathematical techniques.
- Consensus Mechanisms → Algorithms enabling agreement on ledger state across distributed nodes.
- Decentralization → Absence of a central authority, distributing control across the network.
- Peer-to-Peer Network → Direct interaction between participants without intermediaries.
- Smart Contracts → Self-executing agreements with terms directly coded.
- Tokenization → Representing real-world assets as digital tokens on a blockchain.
