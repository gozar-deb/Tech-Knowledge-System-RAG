## Quick Definition
Video Understanding is a subfield of computer vision focused on enabling machines to analyze and interpret dynamic visual information from video sequences. It aims to comprehend actions, events, and temporal relationships within scenes, extracting semantic meaning from continuous visual data.

## Key Concepts
- Action Recognition → Identifying specific human movements or activities.
- Object Tracking → Following the trajectory of objects across video frames.
- Scene Understanding → Interpreting the overall context and environment of a video.
- Temporal Modeling → Analyzing time-series data to capture dynamic patterns.
- Event Detection → Recognizing significant occurrences or incidents in video.
- Video Summarization → Condensing long videos into short, informative clips.
