## Quick Definition
ERC Token Standards are a set of technical rules on the Ethereum blockchain that govern the creation and behavior of digital tokens. They ensure interoperability, allowing tokens to interact seamlessly with various decentralized applications, wallets, and exchanges within the Ethereum ecosystem.

## Key Concepts
- ERC-20 → Standard for fungible tokens, interchangeable and used for currencies or utility.
- ERC-721 → Standard for non-fungible tokens (NFTs), unique and representing distinct assets.
- ERC-1155 → Multi-token standard supporting fungible, non-fungible, and semi-fungible tokens.
- Smart Contract → Code that defines token logic, governing minting, transfers, and burning.
- Interoperability → Ability of tokens to function across different platforms and applications.
- Tokenomics → The study of a token's economic model, including supply, distribution, and utility.
