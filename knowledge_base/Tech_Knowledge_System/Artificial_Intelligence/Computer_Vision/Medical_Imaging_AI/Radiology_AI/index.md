## Quick Definition
Radiology AI applies artificial intelligence, particularly machine learning and deep learning, to medical imaging for enhanced interpretation, analysis, and diagnostic support. It aims to improve accuracy, efficiency, and patient outcomes in radiological workflows by automating and optimizing various imaging tasks.

## Key Concepts
- Machine Learning (ML) → Algorithms trained on data to identify patterns and make predictions.
- Deep Learning (DL) → A subset of ML using neural networks for complex pattern recognition, especially in images.
- Computer-Aided Diagnosis (CAD) → Software that assists radiologists in detecting abnormalities and making diagnoses.
- Image Segmentation → Isolating specific structures or regions of interest within medical images.
- Image Reconstruction → Generating high-quality images from raw data, often improving speed or reducing radiation.
- Quantitative Imaging → Extracting measurable data from medical images for objective assessment and monitoring.
- Workflow Optimization → Streamlining radiological processes from image acquisition to reporting.
- Explainable AI (XAI) → AI models designed to be interpretable and transparent to human users, fostering trust.
