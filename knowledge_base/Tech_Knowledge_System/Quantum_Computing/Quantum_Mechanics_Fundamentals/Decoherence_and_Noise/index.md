## Quick Definition
Decoherence is the process by which a quantum system loses its quantum properties due to interaction with its environment, transitioning towards classical behavior. Quantum noise refers to random perturbations that introduce errors into quantum states, collectively posing the most significant challenge to scalable quantum computation.

## Key Concepts
- Decoherence → Loss of quantum coherence due to environmental interaction.
- Quantum Noise → Random fluctuations perturbing quantum states and operations.
- Qubit Lifetime → Duration a qubit maintains its quantum state before degradation.
- Dephasing → Loss of relative phase information between superposition states.
- Relaxation → Energy loss from an excited qubit to its environment.
- Quantum Error Correction → Techniques to protect quantum information from noise.
