## Quick Definition
Gene Regulatory Networks (GRNs) are complex systems of interacting molecular components that control gene expression within a cell. They orchestrate the precise timing and levels of gene activation and repression, dictating cellular functions, development, and responses to environmental cues.

## Key Concepts
- Gene Expression → The process by which genetic information is used to synthesize functional gene products.
- Transcription Factors → Proteins that bind to specific DNA sequences to regulate gene transcription.
- Regulatory Elements → DNA sequences that control gene expression, often binding sites for transcription factors.
- Network Inference → Computational methods to reconstruct GRN structure from experimental data.
- Feedback Loops → Regulatory mechanisms where a network\'s output influences its own input, crucial for stability and dynamics.
- Modularity → The organization of GRNs into functional units, allowing for robust and adaptable control.
