## Quick Definition
AI Agents and Autonomy refers to intelligent systems that can perceive their environment, make decisions, and execute actions independently to achieve defined goals. They operate with varying degrees of self-governance, minimizing the need for continuous human intervention.

## Key Concepts
- Agent → An entity that perceives and acts within an environment.
- Autonomy → The capacity of an agent to operate independently without constant human oversight.
- Multi-Agent Systems → A collection of interacting autonomous agents working towards common or individual objectives.
- Perception → The process by which an AI agent interprets sensory data from its surroundings.
- Reasoning → The logical process an AI agent uses to derive conclusions and inform decisions.
- Planning → The ability of an AI agent to formulate a sequence of actions to reach a desired state.
- Execution → The act of carrying out planned actions in the real or simulated environment.
- Agentic AI → Emphasizes AI's ability to operate autonomously and perform tasks on its own.
