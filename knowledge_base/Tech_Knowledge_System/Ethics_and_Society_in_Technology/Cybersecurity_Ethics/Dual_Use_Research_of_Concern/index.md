## Quick Definition
Dual Use Research of Concern (DURC) refers to scientific research and technologies that have legitimate beneficial applications but also possess the potential for misuse, leading to significant harm. In cybersecurity, this encompasses tools, techniques, and knowledge that can both enhance security and facilitate malicious activities, posing complex ethical and security challenges.

## Key Concepts
- Intent vs. Impact → Differentiating between a researcher's benign goals and the potential harmful outcomes of their work.
- Responsible Innovation → A framework for developing new technologies while proactively addressing ethical, social, and environmental implications.
- Misuse Potential → The inherent capacity of a technology or research finding to be repurposed for malicious ends.
- Ethical Oversight → The process of reviewing and guiding research to ensure adherence to ethical principles and minimize risks.
- Information Security Dilemma → The challenge of sharing knowledge for progress versus restricting it to prevent harm.
- Risk Mitigation Strategies → Methods and policies designed to reduce the likelihood and impact of DURC misuse.
