## Quick Definition
Digital forensics is the specialized field of recovering and investigating material found in digital devices, often in relation to computer crime. It involves a systematic process to identify, preserve, analyze, and present digital evidence in a legally sound manner.

## Key Concepts
- Chain of Custody → documented process ensuring evidence integrity from collection to presentation
- Volatile Data → information that is lost when a system is powered off, requiring immediate collection
- Disk Imaging → creating a bit-for-bit copy of a storage device for forensic analysis
- Artifact Analysis → examining digital traces left by user or system activity
- Write Blocker → hardware or software preventing accidental modification of evidence drives
- Anti-Forensics → techniques used to obscure or destroy digital evidence
