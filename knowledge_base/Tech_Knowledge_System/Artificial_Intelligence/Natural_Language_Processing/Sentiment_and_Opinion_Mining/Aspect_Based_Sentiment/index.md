## Quick Definition
Aspect-Based Sentiment Analysis (ABSA) is a specialized natural language processing technique that identifies specific aspects within text and determines the sentiment expressed towards each of them, providing granular insights beyond overall document sentiment.

## Key Concepts
- Aspect Extraction → Identifying specific entities or attributes discussed in text.
- Opinion Term Extraction → Pinpointing words or phrases conveying sentiment about aspects.
- Sentiment Polarity Classification → Assigning positive, negative, or neutral sentiment to each aspect.
- Target-Dependent Sentiment Analysis → Analyzing sentiment directly linked to a specific target.
- Fine-grained Opinion Mining → Detailed sentiment analysis at a sub-sentence or aspect level.
- Pre-trained Language Models → Utilizing models like BERT or GPT for enhanced aspect and sentiment detection.
