## Quick Definition
Foundational Concepts in Quantum Computing are the core quantum mechanical principles that enable quantum computation. They encompass phenomena like superposition and entanglement, which are essential for understanding how quantum systems process information and form the basis for quantum algorithms and hardware.

## Key Concepts
- Superposition → Qubits exist in multiple states simultaneously until measured.
- Entanglement → Linked qubits where one's state instantly affects others.
- Qubit → Basic unit of quantum information, can be 0, 1, or both.
- Quantum Gates → Operations manipulating qubit states in quantum circuits.
- Quantum Coherence → Maintenance of quantum properties like superposition and entanglement.
- Decoherence → Loss of quantum properties due to environmental interaction.
