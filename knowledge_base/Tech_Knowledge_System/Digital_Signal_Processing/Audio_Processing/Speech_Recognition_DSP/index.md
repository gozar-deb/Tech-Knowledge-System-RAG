## Quick Definition
Speech Recognition DSP is the application of digital signal processing techniques to convert spoken language into text. It forms the foundation for automatic speech recognition (ASR) systems, enabling machines to understand and process human voice commands and dictation.

## Key Concepts
- Feature Extraction → Process of converting raw audio into numerical features like MFCCs.
- Acoustic Model → Maps extracted audio features to phonetic units or words.
- Language Model → Predicts the probability of word sequences to aid in transcription.
- Hidden Markov Models (HMMs) → Statistical models widely used for sequential data like speech.
- Deep Neural Networks (DNNs) → Modern AI models for enhanced acoustic and language modeling.
- Voice Activity Detection (VAD) → Algorithm to detect presence of human speech in an audio signal.
