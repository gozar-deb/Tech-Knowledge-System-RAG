## Quick Definition
Qubits are the quantum equivalent of classical bits, capable of existing in a superposition of 0 and 1 simultaneously. Superposition is a quantum mechanical principle allowing a system to be in multiple states at once until measured, enabling quantum parallelism.

## Key Concepts
- Qubit → The basic unit of quantum information, existing in a superposition of states.
- Superposition → The ability of a quantum system to be in multiple states concurrently.
- Bloch Sphere → A visual representation of a single qubit's state space.
- Coherence → The property of a quantum system to maintain its superposition and phase relationships.
- Decoherence → The loss of quantum properties due to environmental interaction.
- Measurement → The process that collapses a qubit's superposition into a definite classical outcome.
