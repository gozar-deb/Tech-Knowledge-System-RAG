## Quick Definition
Metaheuristics are high-level algorithmic frameworks that guide the search for optimal solutions in complex optimization problems. They are problem-independent strategies that combine and adapt heuristics to efficiently explore large solution spaces, often when exact methods are computationally prohibitive.

## Key Concepts
- Heuristic → A problem-specific method for finding good, but not necessarily optimal, solutions quickly.
- Metaheuristic → A general strategy that orchestrates and improves the performance of underlying heuristics.
- Exploration → The process of discovering new, unvisited regions of the search space.
- Exploitation → The process of intensifying the search in promising regions to refine existing solutions.
- Local Optima → A solution that is the best within its immediate neighborhood, but not globally.
- Global Optima → The absolute best solution across the entire problem domain.
- Population-based algorithms → Algorithms that work with a collection of solutions, evolving them over time.
- Single-solution algorithms → Algorithms that iteratively improve a single candidate solution.
