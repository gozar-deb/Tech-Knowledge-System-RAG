## Quick Definition
Agentic frameworks are software platforms or toolkits designed for the development, deployment, and management of AI agents. They provide the underlying structure and built-in functionalities that enable AI agents to exhibit autonomous behavior, reason, plan, and execute complex, multi-step workflows.

## Key Concepts
- Agent Orchestration → Managing and coordinating the interactions and tasks of multiple AI agents.
- Tool Integration → Connecting AI agents with external tools, APIs, and services to extend their capabilities.
- Memory Management → Mechanisms for agents to store, retrieve, and utilize past interactions and learned information.
- Workflow Definition → Structuring the sequence of tasks, decisions, and actions an agent needs to perform to achieve a goal.
- Reasoning Loops → Iterative processes where agents perceive, analyze, decide, and act based on their environment and objectives.
- Autonomous Execution → The ability of AI agents to operate independently without constant human intervention.
- Multi-Agent Systems → Architectures involving several AI agents collaborating to solve complex problems or achieve shared goals.
- Guardrails → Safety and ethical constraints implemented to ensure agents operate within defined boundaries.
- LLM Integration → Incorporating Large Language Models as the core reasoning engine for dynamic decision-making.
- Observability → The ability to monitor, understand, and debug the internal state and behavior of AI agents.
