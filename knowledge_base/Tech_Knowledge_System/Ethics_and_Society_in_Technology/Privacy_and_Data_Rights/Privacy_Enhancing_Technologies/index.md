## Quick Definition
Privacy Enhancing Technologies (PETs) are a suite of methods and tools designed to protect personal data during collection, storage, processing, and sharing. They enable data utility while upholding privacy principles, often through cryptographic techniques or data transformation, crucial for regulatory compliance and trust.

## Key Concepts
- Anonymization → Irreversibly removes identifiers from data.
- Pseudonymization → Replaces identifiers with aliases, reversible with extra info.
- Homomorphic Encryption → Computes on encrypted data without decryption.
- Secure Multi-Party Computation (MPC) → Joint computation on private inputs.
- Differential Privacy → Adds noise to data to protect individual privacy.
- Zero-Knowledge Proofs (ZKP) → Proves knowledge without revealing the secret.
- Federated Learning → Distributed machine learning without central data aggregation.
- Data Minimization → Principle of collecting only essential data.
