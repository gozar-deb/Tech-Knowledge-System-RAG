## Quick Definition
Quantum mechanics is the foundational theory in physics that describes the behavior of matter and energy at the atomic and subatomic levels. It introduces concepts like wave-particle duality, superposition, and entanglement, which are essential for understanding quantum phenomena and technologies.

## Key Concepts
- Wave-Particle Duality → Particles exhibit both wave-like and particle-like characteristics.
- Superposition → A quantum system can exist in multiple states simultaneously until measured.
- Entanglement → Two or more particles become linked, sharing correlated fates.
- Uncertainty Principle → It is impossible to precisely know both position and momentum of a particle.
- Quantum States → Mathematical descriptions of a quantum system's properties.
- Quantum Measurement → The act of observing a quantum system, causing its superposition to collapse.
- Quantization → Physical quantities exist in discrete, indivisible units.
- Decoherence → Loss of quantum coherence due to interaction with the environment.
