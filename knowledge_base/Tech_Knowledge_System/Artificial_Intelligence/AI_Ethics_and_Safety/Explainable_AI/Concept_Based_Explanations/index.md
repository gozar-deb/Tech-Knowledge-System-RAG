## Quick Definition
Concept-based explanations in XAI provide human-understandable insights into AI model decisions by linking them to high-level, cognitive concepts. This approach enhances transparency and trust by explaining complex AI behavior in terms familiar to users.

## Key Concepts
- Concept Activation Vectors (CAVs) → Quantify concept presence in model's internal representations.
- Prototype-based Explanations → Explain predictions by identifying similar influential training examples.
- Counterfactual Concepts → Minimal concept changes to alter a model's prediction.
- Rule-based Explanations → Deriving symbolic rules from models for transparent decision logic.
- Concept Bottleneck Models (CBMs) → Models inherently explainable through explicit concept reasoning.
- Disentangled Representations → Learning independent, meaningful concepts for easier explanation.
- Causal Concept Explanations → Identifying causal links between concepts and model outputs.
- Human-in-the-Loop XAI → Integrating human feedback to refine concept-based explanations.
