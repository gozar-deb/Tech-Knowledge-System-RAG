## Quick Definition
Monte Carlo methods are computational algorithms that leverage repeated random sampling to approximate solutions to complex mathematical problems, particularly those intractable by analytical means. They are widely used for simulation, optimization, and numerical integration across various scientific and engineering disciplines.

## Key Concepts
- Random Sampling → Drawing random numbers from a probability distribution to simulate a process.
- Probability Distribution → Describes the likelihood of different outcomes in a random experiment.
- Law of Large Numbers → States that sample mean converges to expected value as sample size increases.
- Variance Reduction → Techniques to improve the efficiency and accuracy of Monte Carlo simulations.
- Markov Chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) → Algorithms for sampling from complex probability distributions.
- Pseudo-random Number Generators → Algorithms producing sequences that mimic true randomness for reproducibility.
- Stochastic Processes → Mathematical models for systems evolving randomly over time.
