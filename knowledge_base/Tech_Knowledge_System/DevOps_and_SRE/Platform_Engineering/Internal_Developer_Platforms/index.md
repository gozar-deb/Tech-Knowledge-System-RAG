## Quick Definition
Internal Developer Platforms (IDPs) provide a self-service layer that abstracts infrastructure complexity, enabling developers to rapidly build, deploy, and operate applications. They standardize development workflows, reduce cognitive load, and enforce organizational governance, acting as a "paved road" for software delivery.

## Key Concepts
- Self-Service Infrastructure → Developers provision resources on-demand without manual intervention.
- Platform Orchestrator → Automates infrastructure provisioning and deployment based on developer requests.
- Internal Developer Portal → Centralized interface for discovering services, documentation, and managing deployments.
- Golden Paths → Opinionated, automated workflows guiding developers to best practices.
- Cognitive Load Reduction → Minimizing infrastructure knowledge required from developers.
- Platform as a Product → Treating the IDP as a product with developers as its primary customers.
