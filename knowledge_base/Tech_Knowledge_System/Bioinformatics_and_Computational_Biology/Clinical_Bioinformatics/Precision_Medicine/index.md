## Quick Definition
Precision medicine is an innovative approach to disease treatment and prevention that considers individual variability in genes, environment, and lifestyle for each person. It aims to tailor medical decisions, treatments, practices, and products to the individual patient.

## Key Concepts
- Personalized Healthcare → Tailoring medical treatment to individual patient characteristics.
- Genomic Sequencing → Determining the complete DNA sequence of an organism's genome.
- Biomarker Discovery → Identifying biological indicators for disease presence or treatment response.
- Targeted Therapy → Drugs designed to act on specific molecular targets involved in disease.
- Pharmacogenomics → Studying how genes affect a person's response to drugs.
- Data Integration → Combining diverse datasets (genomic, clinical, lifestyle) for comprehensive analysis.
