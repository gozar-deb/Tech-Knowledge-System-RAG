## Quick Definition
Attention and perception are core cognitive processes in Human-Computer Interaction (HCI). Attention is the selective focus of mental resources on specific stimuli, while perception is the interpretation of sensory information from interfaces. Together, they dictate how users engage with and understand digital systems.

## Key Concepts
- Selective Attention → Focusing on specific information while filtering out distractions.
- Divided Attention → Distributing attention across multiple tasks or stimuli simultaneously.
- Sustained Attention → Maintaining focus on a task over an extended period.
- Cognitive Load → The total amount of mental effort being used in the working memory.
- Gestalt Principles → Rules describing how humans perceive objects as organized patterns or wholes.
- Change Blindness → Failure to notice changes in a visual scene when attention is directed elsewhere.
