## Quick Definition
Text-to-Image (T2I) models are a class of generative artificial intelligence that synthesize novel images from natural language descriptions. They interpret textual prompts to create corresponding visual content, bridging linguistic understanding with visual generation capabilities. [1] [2]

## Key Concepts
- Prompt Engineering → Crafting effective text inputs to guide image generation.
- Latent Space → A compressed, abstract representation where image features are encoded.
- Diffusion Models → Generative models that iteratively refine noise into coherent images.
- Text Encoder → Translates natural language prompts into numerical embeddings for the model.
- Image Decoder → Converts latent representations back into high-resolution visual outputs.
- Cross-Attention Mechanism → Allows the model to focus on relevant text tokens during image synthesis.
