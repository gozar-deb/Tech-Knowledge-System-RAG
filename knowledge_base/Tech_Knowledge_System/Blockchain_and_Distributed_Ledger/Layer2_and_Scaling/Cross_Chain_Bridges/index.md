## Quick Definition
Cross-chain bridges are protocols enabling the transfer of assets and data between distinct blockchain networks. They facilitate interoperability, allowing otherwise incompatible blockchains to communicate and share value, crucial for scaling and expanding the utility of decentralized ecosystems.

## Key Concepts
- **Interoperability** → The ability of different blockchain networks to communicate and exchange data or assets.
- **Wrapped Assets** → A token on one blockchain that represents an asset on another blockchain, maintaining its value.
- **Relay Networks** → Systems that monitor events on one chain and relay them to another, often with cryptographic proofs.
- **Liquidity Pools** → Pools of tokens locked in smart contracts to facilitate asset swaps and transfers across chains.
- **Trusted Bridges** → Bridges relying on a centralized entity or a small set of validators for security and operation.
- **Trustless Bridges** → Bridges that use cryptographic proofs and decentralized mechanisms to ensure security without relying on intermediaries.
- **Atomic Swaps** → Peer-to-peer exchange of cryptocurrencies from different blockchains without the need for an intermediary.
- **Light Clients** → Nodes that download only block headers and verify transactions using simplified payment verification (SPV).
