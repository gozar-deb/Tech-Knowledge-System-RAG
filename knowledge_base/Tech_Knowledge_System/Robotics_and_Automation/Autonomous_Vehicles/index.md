## Quick Definition
Autonomous vehicles are self-driving systems that utilize a combination of sensors, artificial intelligence, and control algorithms to navigate and operate without human input. They aim to revolutionize transportation by improving safety, efficiency, and accessibility.

## Key Concepts
- Perception → The ability to interpret sensory data (Lidar, Radar, Camera) to understand the environment.
- Localization → Determining the vehicle's precise global and local position.
- Path Planning → Generating a safe, legal, and efficient route for the vehicle to follow.
- Control Systems → Actuating the vehicle's steering, braking, and acceleration based on planned trajectories.
- Sensor Fusion → Integrating data from multiple sensors to create a more robust and accurate environmental model.
- Operational Design Domain (ODD) → The specific conditions under which an autonomous driving system is designed to function.
