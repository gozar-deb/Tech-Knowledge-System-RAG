## Quick Definition
Video Generation Models are a subset of generative AI that create new video content from various inputs like text, images, or other videos. They leverage advanced deep learning architectures to synthesize realistic and coherent motion sequences, often building upon techniques from image generation.

## Key Concepts
- Diffusion Models → Iteratively refine noise into coherent video frames.
- Spatio-Temporal Attention → Allows models to understand relationships across both space (within frames) and time (between frames).
- Latent Space → A compressed representation where video properties are manipulated before generation.
- Text-to-Video → Generating video content directly from natural language descriptions.
- Video-to-Video → Transforming existing video content based on specific prompts or styles.
- Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs) → A framework where two neural networks compete to generate realistic data.
- Transformers → Architectures that process sequential data, crucial for long-range dependencies in video.
- World Simulators → Advanced models capable of generating complex, interactive environments.
