## Quick Definition
Vision Language Models (VLMs) are AI systems that combine computer vision and natural language processing to understand and generate content across both image and text modalities. They enable tasks like describing images, answering questions about visual data, and generating images from text descriptions, bridging the gap between human language and visual perception.

## Key Concepts
- Multimodal AI → AI systems capable of processing and relating information from multiple data types.
- Cross-modal learning → Training models to understand relationships between different data modalities.
- Visual Question Answering (VQA) → Answering natural language questions based on the content of an image.
- Image Captioning → Automatically generating descriptive text for an image.
- Text-to-Image Generation → Creating images from textual descriptions.
- Joint embedding space → A shared representation space where visual and textual data can be compared.
- Attention mechanisms → Allowing models to focus on relevant parts of both visual and textual inputs.
- Transfer learning → Applying knowledge gained from one task to a different but related task, often used with pre-trained VLMs.
