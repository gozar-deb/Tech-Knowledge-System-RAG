## Quick Definition
Image and video generation is a subfield of generative AI focused on creating novel visual content, from static pictures to dynamic sequences, often guided by text or other inputs. These advanced models synthesize realistic and diverse media by learning underlying data distributions.

## Key Concepts
- Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs) → Two neural networks compete to generate realistic data.
- Variational Autoencoders (VAEs) → Learn latent data representations for probabilistic generation.
- Diffusion Models → Iteratively refine noise into coherent images, achieving high fidelity.
- Latent Space Interpolation → Manipulating generated content by navigating a learned feature space.
- Text-to-Image Synthesis → Creating images directly from natural language descriptions.
- Conditional Generation → Guiding output characteristics with specific input conditions.
