## Quick Definition
System prompts are foundational instructions given to large language models (LLMs) to define their overarching behavior, role, and constraints. Persona engineering is the art of crafting these prompts to imbue an LLM with a consistent identity, tone, and knowledge, ensuring controlled and contextually appropriate AI interactions.

## Key Concepts
- Meta-Instructions → High-level directives governing LLM behavior.
- Role-Playing → Assigning a specific identity to an LLM.
- Behavioral Constraints → Defining rules and boundaries for LLM output.
- Context Window → The limited input space where prompts and conversation history reside.
- Instruction Tuning → Fine-tuning LLMs to better follow complex instructions.
- AI Alignment → Steering LLM behavior towards desired ethical and functional outcomes.
- Prompt Injection → Malicious input bypassing system instructions.
- Persona Consistency → Maintaining a coherent identity throughout interaction.
