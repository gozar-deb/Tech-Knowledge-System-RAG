## Quick Definition
Emerging trends in quantum computing represent the forefront of quantum technology development, focusing on innovative hardware, advanced algorithms, and novel applications. These trends aim to overcome current limitations and unlock the full potential of quantum advantage across various domains.

## Key Concepts
- Quantum Annealing → a quantum optimization method for complex problems
- Topological Quantum Computing → uses topological properties for robust quantum information
- Quantum Machine Learning → applies quantum algorithms to machine learning tasks
- Quantum Internet → a network for secure quantum communication and distributed computing
- Quantum Sensing → uses quantum mechanics for highly precise measurements
- Quantum Cryptography → provides unbreakable security through quantum principles
