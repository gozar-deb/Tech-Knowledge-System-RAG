## Quick Definition
Foundational Concepts in Artificial Intelligence (AI) refer to the core theories, principles, and paradigms that form the bedrock of intelligent system design. These concepts include problem-solving, knowledge representation, reasoning, and learning, enabling the development of diverse AI applications.

## Key Concepts
- Symbolic AI → Focuses on explicit representation of knowledge and logical reasoning.
- Connectionism → Emphasizes neural networks and learning from data patterns.
- Search Algorithms → Methods for exploring possible solutions to problems, like A* or minimax.
- Knowledge Representation → Techniques for encoding information about the world for AI systems.
- Machine Learning → Algorithms allowing systems to learn from data without explicit programming.
- Reasoning → The process of drawing conclusions from existing knowledge and rules.
- Problem Solving → Strategies for achieving goals, often involving search and planning.
- Ethics in AI → Principles guiding responsible development and deployment of AI systems.
