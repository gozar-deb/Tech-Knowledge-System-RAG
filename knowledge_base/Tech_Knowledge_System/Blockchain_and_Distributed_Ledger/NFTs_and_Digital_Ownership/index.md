## Quick Definition
Non-Fungible Tokens (NFTs) are unique blockchain-based tokens representing ownership of specific digital or physical assets. They enable verifiable scarcity and provenance, transforming how digital items are owned, traded, and valued in the digital economy.

## Key Concepts
- Non-Fungibility → Uniqueness and non-interchangeability of assets.
- Smart Contracts → Automated agreements governing NFT creation and transfer.
- Token Standards → Rulesets like ERC-721 and ERC-1155 for NFT functionality.
- Digital Scarcity → Verifiable limitation of digital asset quantities.
- Provenance → On-chain record of an NFT's ownership history.
- Metadata → Descriptive data linked to an NFT, often stored off-chain.
