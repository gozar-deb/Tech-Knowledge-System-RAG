## Quick Definition
EU AI Act Compliance involves adhering to the European Union's landmark Artificial Intelligence Act, which regulates AI systems based on their potential risk. It mandates stringent requirements for high-risk AI, focusing on safety, transparency, human oversight, and fundamental rights protection across the AI lifecycle.

## Key Concepts
- Risk-Based Approach → Classifies AI systems by risk level (unacceptable, high, limited, minimal).
- High-Risk AI Systems → AI in critical sectors requiring strict compliance and conformity assessments.
- Conformity Assessment → Process to verify high-risk AI systems meet regulatory standards before market entry.
- Post-Market Monitoring → Ongoing surveillance of deployed AI systems to ensure continued adherence to the Act.
- Transparency Obligations → Requirements for clear communication about AI system capabilities and limitations.
- Human Oversight → Mandates for human control and intervention in high-risk AI applications.
