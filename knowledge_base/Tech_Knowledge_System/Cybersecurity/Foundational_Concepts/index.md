## Quick Definition
Foundational Concepts in Cybersecurity establish the essential principles, models, and terminology required to understand and manage information security. This node covers core ideas like confidentiality, integrity, availability, and risk management, forming the bedrock for all advanced cybersecurity knowledge and practices.

## Key Concepts
- Confidentiality → Protecting information from unauthorized access.
- Integrity → Ensuring data accuracy and preventing unauthorized modification.
- Availability → Guaranteeing reliable access to systems and data for authorized users.
- Risk Management → The process of identifying, assessing, and mitigating security risks.
- Threat Modeling → A structured approach to identify potential threats and vulnerabilities.
- Security Controls → Measures implemented to protect assets and reduce risks.
