## Quick Definition
Human-Computer Interaction (HCI) is the study of how people interact with computers and, to a greater extent, how to make computers more user-friendly. It combines computer science, psychology, and design to create effective and enjoyable user experiences.

## Key Concepts
- User Experience (UX) → The holistic experience of a user interacting with a product or service.
- User Interface (UI) → The visual and interactive elements of a product that a user engages with.
- Usability → The degree to which a product can be used by specified users to achieve specified goals with effectiveness, efficiency, and satisfaction.
- Accessibility → Designing products to be usable by people with the widest range of abilities.
- Interaction Design → Shaping the way people interact with products and services.
- User-Centered Design (UCD) → A design philosophy and process that prioritizes the needs and goals of the end-users.
