## Quick Definition
Emerging trends in technology ethics and society explore the evolving moral dilemmas and social consequences stemming from rapid technological advancements. This field critically examines how innovations like AI, biotechnology, and quantum computing necessitate new ethical frameworks and reshape human values, governance, and societal structures.

## Key Concepts
- Algorithmic Bias → Unfair outcomes from algorithms due to skewed data or design.
- Data Sovereignty → National control over data collected or stored within its borders.
- Digital Divide → Disparity in access to information and communication technologies.
- Responsible Innovation → Proactive assessment and societal engagement in technology development.
- Ethical AI → Designing and deploying AI systems aligned with moral principles.
- Technology Governance → Policies and regulations guiding new technology development.
