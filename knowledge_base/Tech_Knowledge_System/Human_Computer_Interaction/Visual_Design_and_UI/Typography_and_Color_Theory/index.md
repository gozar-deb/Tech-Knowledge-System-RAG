## Quick Definition
Typography is the strategic arrangement of text for optimal legibility and aesthetic appeal, while color theory provides principles for effective color combination and usage. Together, they form the bedrock of visual design, profoundly influencing user experience and interface effectiveness.

## Key Concepts
- Legibility → Ease of distinguishing individual characters and words.
- Readability → How easily text can be read and understood.
- Typographic Hierarchy → Visual organization of text to guide the reader's eye.
- Color Harmony → Visually pleasing combinations of colors.
- Contrast Ratio → Numerical measure of difference between foreground and background colors.
- Accessibility → Designing for users with diverse abilities, including color blindness and low vision.
