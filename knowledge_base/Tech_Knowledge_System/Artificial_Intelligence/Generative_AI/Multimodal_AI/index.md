## Quick Definition
Multimodal AI integrates and processes diverse data types like text, images, and audio to achieve a more holistic understanding of information. It enables AI systems to perceive and interact with the world in a manner closer to human cognition, leading to more robust and versatile applications.

## Key Concepts
- Modality → A distinct form of data, such as text, image, or audio.
- Data Fusion → Combining information from multiple data types.
- Cross-Modal Understanding → Interpreting relationships between different data modalities.
- Representation Learning → Developing effective data representations for multimodal inputs.
- Alignment → Matching corresponding elements across different modalities.
- Generative Multimodal Models → AI models capable of creating new content across various modalities.
