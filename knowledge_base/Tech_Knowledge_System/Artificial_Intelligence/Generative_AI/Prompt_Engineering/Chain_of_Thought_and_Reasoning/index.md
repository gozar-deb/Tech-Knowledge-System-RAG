## Quick Definition
Chain-of-Thought (CoT) prompting is a technique in prompt engineering that guides Large Language Models (LLMs) to perform complex reasoning by generating a sequence of intermediate, logical steps. This method significantly improves the accuracy and reliability of LLM outputs for multi-step problems.

## Key Concepts
- Chain of Thought (CoT) → A series of explicit intermediate reasoning steps generated by an LLM.
- Prompt Engineering → The discipline of designing and optimizing prompts for LLMs.
- Large Language Models (LLMs) → AI models capable of understanding and generating human-like text.
- Reasoning → The process of drawing conclusions from premises or evidence.
- Problem Decomposition → Breaking down a complex problem into smaller, more manageable parts.
- Few-shot CoT → Providing examples of step-by-step reasoning within the prompt.
- Zero-shot CoT → Eliciting CoT reasoning without examples, often with a simple instruction.
- Self-consistency → Generating multiple CoT paths and selecting the most frequent answer.
