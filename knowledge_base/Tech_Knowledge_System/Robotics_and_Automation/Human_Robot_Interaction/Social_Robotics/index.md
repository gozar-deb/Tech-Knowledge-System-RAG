## Quick Definition
Social robotics is a specialized field focused on creating robots that can interact and communicate with humans in a socially intelligent and natural manner. These robots are designed to understand and respond to human social cues, fostering engaging and often empathetic relationships to enhance user experience.

## Key Concepts
- Human-Robot Interaction (HRI) → The study of how humans and robots interact, focusing on design and understanding.
- Social Intelligence → A robot's ability to interpret and respond to human social and emotional signals.
- Embodiment → The physical form of a robot and its influence on human perception and interaction.
- Affective Computing → Systems that recognize, interpret, and simulate human emotions.
- Trust and Acceptance → User willingness to engage with and rely on social robots.
- Theory of Mind (ToM) → A robot's capacity to infer and model human mental states.
