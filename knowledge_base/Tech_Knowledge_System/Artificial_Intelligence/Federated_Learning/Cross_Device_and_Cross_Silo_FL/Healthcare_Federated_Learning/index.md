## Quick Definition
Healthcare Federated Learning (FL) is a distributed machine learning approach that enables multiple healthcare organizations to collaboratively train AI models without sharing raw patient data. This method preserves data privacy and security while leveraging diverse datasets for robust model development in medical applications.

## Key Concepts
- Data Privacy: Protecting sensitive patient information by keeping it localized at its source.
- Collaborative AI: Joint model training across institutions without direct data exchange.
- Model Aggregation: Combining local model updates into a global, improved AI model.
- Non-IID Data: Addressing statistical heterogeneity in data distributions across different healthcare sites.
- Secure Multi-Party Computation: Cryptographic techniques to ensure privacy during model aggregation.
- Regulatory Compliance: Adherence to healthcare data protection laws like HIPAA and GDPR.
- Personalized Medicine: Developing tailored treatments by training models on diverse patient cohorts.
