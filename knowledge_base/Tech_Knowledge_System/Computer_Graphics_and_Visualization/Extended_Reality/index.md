## Quick Definition
Extended Reality (XR) is an umbrella term encompassing Virtual Reality (VR), Augmented Reality (AR), and Mixed Reality (MR). It describes immersive technologies that merge the physical and digital worlds, creating interactive experiences along a spectrum of immersion from fully virtual to augmented reality.

## Key Concepts
- Virtual Reality (VR) → fully immersive digital environments replacing the real world
- Augmented Reality (AR) → overlays digital information onto the real world
- Mixed Reality (MR) → blends real and virtual worlds where objects co-exist and interact
- Immersion → subjective feeling of being present in a non-physical world
- Presence → psychological state of feeling actually in the virtual environment
- Haptics → technology simulating tactile feedback for virtual interactions
