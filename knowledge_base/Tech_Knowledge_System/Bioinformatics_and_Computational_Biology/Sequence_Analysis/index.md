## Quick Definition
Sequence analysis is the computational process of examining DNA, RNA, or protein sequences to uncover biological insights. It involves comparing sequences, identifying functional elements, and inferring evolutionary relationships, forming a cornerstone of modern molecular biology and bioinformatics.

## Key Concepts
- Sequence Alignment → Comparing sequences to find similarities and evolutionary relationships.
- Homology → Shared ancestry between sequences or structures.
- Phylogenetics → Studying evolutionary history using molecular data.
- Motif Discovery → Finding conserved patterns with biological significance.
- Gene Prediction → Identifying genes and functional regions in genomes.
- Next-Generation Sequencing (NGS) → High-throughput DNA sequencing technologies.
