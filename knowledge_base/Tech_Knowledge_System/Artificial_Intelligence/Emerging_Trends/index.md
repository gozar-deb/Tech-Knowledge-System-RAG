## Quick Definition
Emerging trends in Artificial Intelligence represent the forefront of innovation, encompassing new paradigms, algorithms, and applications that are rapidly shaping the future of the field. These areas often push the boundaries of current AI capabilities, addressing complex challenges and opening up novel opportunities across various domains.

## Key Concepts
- Generative AI → AI models that create new content like text, images, or code.
- Explainable AI (XAI) → Systems designed to make AI decisions understandable to humans.
- Federated Learning → Distributed machine learning that trains models on decentralized data.
- Quantum AI → Integration of quantum computing with AI for enhanced processing power.
- Responsible AI → Ethical and safe development and deployment of AI systems.
- Neuromorphic Computing → Hardware mimicking brain structure for efficient AI.
- Edge AI → Running AI models directly on local devices for real-time inference.
- Foundation Models → Large, pre-trained models adaptable to many tasks.
