## Quick Definition
Software architecture is the high-level structure of a software system, defining its components, their external properties, and their relationships. It serves as a blueprint for development, guiding decisions on technology, design patterns, and quality attributes to meet both functional and non-functional requirements.

## Key Concepts
- Architectural Patterns → Reusable solutions for common design problems.
- Quality Attributes → Non-functional requirements like scalability, security, and performance.
- Microservices → An architectural style structuring an application as a collection of loosely coupled services.
- Domain-Driven Design → Focuses on aligning software design with the business domain.
- Technical Debt → The cost of future rework due to quick, suboptimal solutions.
- System Design → The process of defining a system's architecture, modules, and interfaces.
