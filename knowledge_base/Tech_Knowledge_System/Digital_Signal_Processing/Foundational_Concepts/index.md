## Quick Definition
Foundational Concepts in Digital Signal Processing (DSP) cover the essential theories and mathematical methods for analyzing and manipulating discrete-time signals, including analog-to-digital conversion, sampling, quantization, and fundamental signal operations.

## Key Concepts
- Sampling → converting continuous analog signals to discrete-time signals by taking measurements at regular intervals.
- Quantization → mapping continuous amplitude values to a finite set of discrete values, introducing error.
- Aliasing → distortion where high-frequency components appear as lower frequencies due to insufficient sampling rate.
- Nyquist Theorem → specifies the minimum sampling rate (twice the highest frequency) required for perfect signal reconstruction.
- Discrete-Time Systems → systems that process discrete-time signals, often described by difference equations.
- Fourier Transform → mathematical tool to analyze a signal's frequency components, transforming it from time to frequency domain.
