## Quick Definition
Automation and labor explores the multifaceted impact of technological advancements, particularly AI and robotics, on employment, job structures, and the broader economy. It analyzes challenges like job displacement and opportunities for new job creation, alongside the ethical and societal implications of an increasingly automated workforce.

## Key Concepts
- Job Displacement → Loss of jobs due to machines performing tasks previously done by humans.
- Workforce Transformation → Changes in skills, roles, and organizational structures driven by automation.
- Human-Robot Collaboration (HRC) → Humans and robots working together to achieve common goals.
- Reskilling/Upskilling → Training workers for new or advanced skills required by evolving job markets.
- Technological Unemployment → Long-term joblessness caused by technology making human labor obsolete.
- Gig Economy → A labor market based on short-term contracts or freelance work.
- Algorithmic Bias → Unfair or discriminatory outcomes from automated decision-making systems.
