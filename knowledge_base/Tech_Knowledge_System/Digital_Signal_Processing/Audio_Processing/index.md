## Quick Definition
Audio processing is the digital manipulation of sound signals for analysis, enhancement, synthesis, and compression. It forms the backbone of modern audio technology, enabling applications from music production to speech recognition and telecommunications.

## Key Concepts
- Sampling → Conversion of analog sound waves into digital data points.
- Quantization → Assigning discrete numerical values to sampled audio amplitudes.
- Digital Filtering → Modifying frequency components of a digital audio signal.
- Fourier Transform → Analyzing a signal's frequency content over time.
- Audio Compression → Reducing file size while preserving perceived quality.
- Spectral Analysis → Studying the frequency spectrum of audio signals.
- Time-Domain Processing → Direct manipulation of the audio waveform.
- Frequency-Domain Processing → Manipulation of a signal's spectral representation.
