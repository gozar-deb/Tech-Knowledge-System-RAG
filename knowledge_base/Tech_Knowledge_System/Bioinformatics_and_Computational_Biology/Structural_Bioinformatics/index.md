## Quick Definition
Structural bioinformatics is a specialized field that uses computational approaches to analyze, predict, and visualize the three-dimensional structures of biological macromolecules. It is essential for understanding molecular function, interactions, and for applications in drug discovery and biotechnology.

## Key Concepts
- Protein Structure Prediction → Determining a protein's 3D shape from its amino acid sequence.
- Molecular Docking → Simulating how molecules bind to each other, crucial for drug design.
- Molecular Dynamics → Simulating atomic movements over time to observe molecular behavior.
- Homology Modeling → Building a 3D protein model based on a known similar protein structure.
- Structure-Based Drug Design → Designing drugs by targeting specific molecular structures.
- Conformational Changes → How molecular structures change shape in response to stimuli.
