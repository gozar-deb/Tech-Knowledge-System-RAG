## Quick Definition
Emerging trends in robotics and automation encompass the latest advancements and innovations shaping the future of intelligent machines and automated processes. These trends drive efficiency, expand capabilities, and integrate robotics into new domains, transforming industries and daily life.

## Key Concepts
- AI & Autonomy → Integration of artificial intelligence for enhanced decision-making and self-governance in robotic systems.
- Collaborative Robots (Cobots) → Robots designed to work safely and interactively alongside humans in shared workspaces.
- Digital Twins → Virtual replicas of physical robotic systems used for simulation, monitoring, and optimization.
- Industrial Internet of Things (IIoT) → Network of interconnected sensors, instruments, and other devices for industrial applications, enabling data exchange and automation.
- Edge Computing → Processing data closer to the source of generation, reducing latency and improving real-time decision-making for robots.
- Sustainable Robotics → Development and deployment of robots with a focus on environmental impact, energy efficiency, and resource conservation.
