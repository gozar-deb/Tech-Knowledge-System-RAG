## Quick Definition
AlphaFold and ESMFold are state-of-the-art artificial intelligence models that predict the three-dimensional structures of proteins directly from their amino acid sequences. These models have revolutionized structural biology by providing highly accurate predictions, significantly accelerating research in areas like drug discovery and protein engineering.

## Key Concepts
- Deep Learning → Core AI methodology enabling complex pattern recognition for protein folding.
- Protein Language Models → ESMFold's approach, learning protein properties from vast sequence data.
- Evolutionary Information → AlphaFold's method, leveraging multiple sequence alignments to infer structural constraints.
- Protein Folding → The physical process by which a protein chain acquires its intricate 3D structure.
- Amino Acid Sequence → The linear chain of amino acids that determines a protein's final structure.
- Structural Bioinformatics → Field focusing on the computational analysis and prediction of protein structures.
