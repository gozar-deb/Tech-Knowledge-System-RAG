## Quick Definition
The MITRE ATT&CK Framework is a comprehensive, globally accessible knowledge base that catalogs adversary tactics and techniques observed in real-world cyberattacks. It serves as a foundational resource for understanding, detecting, and mitigating cyber threats by providing a common language for describing adversary behaviors.

## Key Concepts
- Tactics → Adversary's strategic goals (e.g., Initial Access, Execution)
- Techniques → Specific methods adversaries use to achieve tactics (e.g., Phishing, Valid Accounts)
- Sub-techniques → More granular descriptions of techniques
- Procedures → Concrete implementations of techniques by specific threat actors
- Matrix → Visual organization of tactics and techniques across different operating environments
- Threat Intelligence → Information about adversaries and their TTPs, often mapped to ATT&CK
