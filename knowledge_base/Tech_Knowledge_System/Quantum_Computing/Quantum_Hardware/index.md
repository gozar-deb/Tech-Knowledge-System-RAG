## Quick Definition
Quantum hardware comprises the physical components and systems essential for building quantum computers. It leverages quantum mechanical principles like superposition and entanglement to process information, offering computational capabilities beyond classical systems.

## Key Concepts
- Qubit → The fundamental unit of quantum information, existing in multiple states simultaneously.
- Superposition → A quantum state allowing a qubit to be in a combination of states until measured.
- Entanglement → A quantum phenomenon where linked qubits share states, influencing each other instantly.
- Quantum Gates → Operations that manipulate qubit states, analogous to classical logic gates.
- Quantum Coherence → The ability of a quantum system to maintain its quantum properties for computation.
- Decoherence → The loss of quantum properties due to environmental interaction, a primary challenge.
