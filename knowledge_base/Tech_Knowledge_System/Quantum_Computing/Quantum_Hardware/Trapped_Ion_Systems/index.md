## Quick Definition
Trapped ion systems are a quantum computing paradigm utilizing electromagnetically confined atomic ions as qubits. Lasers precisely control these ions for quantum operations, offering high-fidelity gates and long coherence times, making them a leading platform for quantum information processing.

## Key Concepts
- Ion Trap → Device confining charged atoms using electromagnetic fields.
- Qubit → An ion's electronic state representing quantum information.
- Laser Cooling → Reducing ion motion to minimize decoherence.
- Quantum Gates → Laser-driven operations on qubits.
- Entanglement → Quantum correlation between multiple qubits.
- Decoherence → Loss of quantum properties due to environmental interaction.
- Readout → Measuring qubit states via fluorescence.
- Quantum Simulation → Modeling complex systems with controllable quantum hardware.
