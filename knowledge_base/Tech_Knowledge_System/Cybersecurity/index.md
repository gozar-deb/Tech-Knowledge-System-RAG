## Quick Definition
Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting computer systems, networks, and data from digital attacks, damage, or unauthorized access. It ensures the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of information assets against a wide range of threats.

## Key Concepts
- CIA Triad → Fundamental principles of information security: Confidentiality, Integrity, Availability.
- Threat Intelligence → Data-driven knowledge about existing or emerging threats to inform defense strategies.
- Vulnerability Assessment → Process of identifying and quantifying security weaknesses in systems.
- Incident Response → Structured approach to manage and recover from security breaches.
- Encryption → Transforming information to secure it from unauthorized access.
- Authentication → Verifying the identity of a user, process, or device.
- Authorization → Determining what an authenticated entity is permitted to do.
- Security Audit → Systematic evaluation of an organization's security posture.
