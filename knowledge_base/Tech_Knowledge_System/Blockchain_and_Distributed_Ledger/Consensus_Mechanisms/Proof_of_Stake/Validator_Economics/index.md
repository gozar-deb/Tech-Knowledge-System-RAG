## Quick Definition
Validator economics in Proof of Stake (PoS) systems refer to the financial incentives and disincentives that govern the behavior of network validators. This includes staking rewards, slashing penalties, and delegation mechanisms, all designed to secure the blockchain and maintain consensus through economic alignment.

## Key Concepts
- Staking → Committing crypto as collateral for validation.
- Rewards → Financial incentives for honest block validation.
- Slashing → Penalties for malicious or negligent validator actions.
- Delegation → Token holders assigning stake to a validator.
- Capital Efficiency → Optimizing staked capital for network security.
- Incentive Alignment → Designing economics to promote desired validator behavior.
