## Quick Definition
Artificial Intelligence (AI) is a field of computer science dedicated to creating machines that can perform tasks typically requiring human intelligence. It involves developing algorithms and systems capable of learning, reasoning, problem-solving, perception, and language understanding.

## Key Concepts
- Machine Learning → Algorithms that allow systems to learn from data.
- Deep Learning → A subset of ML using multi-layered neural networks.
- Natural Language Processing (NLP) → AI's ability to process and understand human language.
- Computer Vision → Enabling computers to interpret and understand visual information.
- Reinforcement Learning → Learning through interaction with an environment to maximize rewards.
- Neural Networks → Computational models inspired by the human brain's structure.
- Generative AI → AI models that can create new, original content.
- Ethical AI → Principles and practices for responsible AI development and deployment.
