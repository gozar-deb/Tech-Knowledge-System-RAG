## Quick Definition
Entropy quantifies the average uncertainty of a random variable, while mutual information measures the shared information between two random variables. These concepts are fundamental in information theory for understanding data dependencies and information content.

## Key Concepts
- Shannon Entropy → Measures the average uncertainty of a random variable.
- Joint Entropy → Uncertainty associated with multiple random variables.
- Conditional Entropy → Uncertainty of one variable given another is known.
- Mutual Information (MI) → Information shared between two variables.
- Kullback-Leibler Divergence → Measures how one probability distribution differs from another.
- Data Processing Inequality → Information cannot be increased by processing data.
