## Quick Definition
Digital Inclusion is the proactive effort to ensure all individuals and communities have equitable access to, and effective use of, information and communication technologies. This encompasses affordable internet, suitable devices, and the necessary digital literacy skills to participate fully in modern society.

## Key Concepts
- Digital Equity → The state where everyone has the technological capacity for full societal participation.
- Digital Divide → The disparity in access to digital technology and skills among different groups.
- Digital Literacy → The ability to effectively navigate, evaluate, and create information using digital platforms.
- Affordable Access → Ensuring internet services and devices are economically viable for all.
- Universal Design → Creating digital products and environments usable by all people, to the greatest extent possible, without the need for adaptation or specialized design.
- Infrastructure Development → Building and maintaining the physical and digital networks required for connectivity.
