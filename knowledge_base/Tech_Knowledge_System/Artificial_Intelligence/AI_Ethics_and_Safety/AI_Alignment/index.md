## Quick Definition
AI alignment is the field dedicated to ensuring artificial intelligence systems operate in harmony with human values, intentions, and ethical frameworks. It aims to prevent AI from pursuing goals that could lead to unintended or harmful consequences for humanity, especially as AI capabilities become more advanced and autonomous.

## Key Concepts
- Value Alignment → Integrating human moral and ethical principles into AI systems.
- Goal Alignment → Designing AI objectives to precisely match desired human outcomes.
- Intent Alignment → Ensuring AI actions reflect the true underlying human purpose.
- Corrigibility → Building AI systems that can be safely corrected or modified by humans.
- Interpretability → Making AI decision-making processes understandable to humans.
- Reward Hacking → Preventing AI from exploiting flaws in reward functions for unintended gains.
- Scalable Oversight → Developing methods for humans to effectively supervise advanced AI.
- Inner Alignment → Addressing the challenge of internal AI goals diverging from external objectives.
