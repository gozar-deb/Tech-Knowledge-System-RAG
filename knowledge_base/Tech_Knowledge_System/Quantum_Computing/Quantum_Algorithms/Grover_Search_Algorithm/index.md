## Quick Definition
Grover's algorithm is a quantum algorithm that provides a quadratic speedup for unstructured search problems. It efficiently finds a unique target item within an unsorted database by leveraging quantum superposition and amplitude amplification, offering a significant advantage over classical search methods.

## Key Concepts
- Unstructured Search → Searching an unsorted database without prior knowledge of its structure.
- Quantum Oracle → A black box function that identifies and marks the target solution.
- Amplitude Amplification → The core quantum technique to boost the probability of measuring the target state.
- Superposition → Qubits exist in multiple states simultaneously, enabling parallel exploration of possibilities.
- Diffusion Operator → A quantum operation that inverts amplitudes around their average, central to amplitude amplification.
- Quantum Speedup → The computational advantage gained by quantum algorithms over their classical counterparts.
- Quantum Gates → Basic building blocks of quantum circuits, analogous to logic gates in classical computing.
