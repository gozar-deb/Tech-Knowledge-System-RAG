## Quick Definition
Just-in-Time (JIT) Access is a security principle and technology that grants temporary, time-bound privileged access to IT resources only when needed. This approach significantly reduces the attack surface by eliminating standing privileges and enforcing the principle of least privilege dynamically.

## Key Concepts
- Temporary Access → Privileges are granted for a limited duration.
- Least Privilege → Users receive only the minimum permissions required for a specific task.
- Dynamic Provisioning → Access is activated on demand, not pre-assigned.
- Automated Revocation → Privileges are automatically removed after use or expiration.
- Approval Workflows → Requests for elevated access often require explicit authorization.
- Session Monitoring → Activities during privileged sessions are typically recorded and audited.
