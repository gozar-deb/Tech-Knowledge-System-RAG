## Quick Definition
User interviews are a qualitative research method where researchers engage in direct conversations with target users to uncover their needs, behaviors, and motivations. This process yields rich, in-depth insights crucial for informing user-centered design and product development decisions.

## Key Concepts
- Semi-structured Interviews → Flexible conversations guided by a protocol but allowing for exploration.
- Open-ended Questions → Questions designed to encourage detailed and reflective responses.
- Active Listening → Focused attention to both verbal and non-verbal communication from users.
- Probing Techniques → Follow-up questions to explore user statements in greater depth.
- Affinity Mapping → A method for synthesizing qualitative data by grouping related insights.
- Empathy Mapping → A collaborative visualization to understand user needs, thoughts, and feelings.
- Interview Protocol → A structured document outlining the interview process and key questions.
- Thematic Analysis → A method for identifying, analyzing, and reporting patterns (themes) within data.
