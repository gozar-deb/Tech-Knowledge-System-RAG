## Quick Definition
Robot Perception is the critical capability for robots to sense, process, and interpret information from their physical environment. It enables autonomous systems to build a coherent understanding of their surroundings, facilitating tasks like navigation, object interaction, and decision-making based on real-world data.

## Key Concepts
- Sensors → Devices like cameras, lidar, and radar for data acquisition.
- Sensor Fusion → Combining data from multiple sensors for robust environmental understanding.
- Object Detection → Identifying and localizing specific objects within sensor data.
- SLAM (Simultaneous Localization and Mapping) → Building a map while simultaneously tracking robot's position.
- Scene Understanding → Interpreting the context and relationships of elements in an environment.
- Pose Estimation → Determining the 3D position and orientation of objects or the robot.
