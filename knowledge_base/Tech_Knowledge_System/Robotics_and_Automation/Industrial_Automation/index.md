## Quick Definition
Industrial automation is the application of control systems, software, and machinery to automate industrial processes, reducing human intervention. It aims to improve efficiency, reliability, and safety in manufacturing and production by controlling operations from individual machines to entire factories.

## Key Concepts
- Programmable Logic Controllers (PLCs) → Digital computers for automating electromechanical processes.
- Distributed Control Systems (DCS) → Control systems for geographically distributed continuous processes.
- Supervisory Control and Data Acquisition (SCADA) → Systems for monitoring and controlling industrial processes.
- Human-Machine Interface (HMI) → Graphical user interfaces for operators to interact with control systems.
- Industrial Internet of Things (IIoT) → Network of interconnected industrial devices, sensors, and software.
- Actuators → Devices that convert control signals into physical motion or action.
- Sensors → Devices that detect and measure physical quantities, converting them into electrical signals.
- Robotics → Use of robots to perform tasks in industrial settings.
